Steps:
1. Transfer 1 µL of BsaI-HF v2 from bsaI_tube to gg_tube. Note: Volumes from the published version of the paper; one reaction shown, repeat for each cassette. [assumed]
2. Transfer 1 µL of T4 DNA ligase from ligase_tube to gg_tube. [assumed]
3. Transfer 2 µL of T4 ligase buffer from ligbuffer_tube to gg_tube. [assumed]
4. Transfer 2 µL of Destination vector (link-0xx) from link_vector_tube to gg_tube. Note: Volume assumed so vector:insert is 1:2 molar. [assumed]
5. Transfer 2 µL of Type-3 intermediate plasmid (gene flanked by BsaI sites) from type3_insert_tube to gg_tube. Note: Volume assumed (insert at 2x molar excess). [assumed]
6. Transfer 12 µL of Water from water_tube to gg_tube. Note: Brings reaction to 20 uL. [assumed]
7. Mix gg_tube by pipetting 10 µL up and down 3 times. Note: Gentle pipette mixing. [assumed]
8. MANUAL: Thermocycle gg_tube: 30 cycles of 5 min at 37 C then 5 min at 16 C. Transform into E. coli DH5alpha, miniprep, verify by Sanger sequencing; linearize inserts by PCR with homology arms and prepare competent yeast cells of the parent strains (5-10 mL culture, OD600 0.5-0.8). Note: Cycling from the published version; PCR linearization and competent cell preparation assumed. [assumed]
9. Transfer 250 µL of 50% PEG3350 from peg_tube to tf_L2_tube. Note: Step 2 transformation (SC.L1 to SC.L2, HIS3::XYL1-3). Conditions from the published version. [assumed]
10. Transfer 36 µL of 1 M LiAc from liac_tube to tf_L2_tube. [assumed]
11. Transfer 10 µL of 2 mg/mL ssDNA from ssdna_tube to tf_L2_tube. [assumed]
12. Transfer 1 µL of pCRCT-HIS3 (250 ng) from pCRCT_HIS3_tube to tf_L2_tube. Note: Stock concentration assumed 250 ng/uL. [assumed]
13. Transfer 4 µL of Linear XYL1-3 insert (2000 ng) from insert_XYL_tube to tf_L2_tube. Note: Stock concentration assumed 500 ng/uL. [assumed]
14. Transfer 59 µL of Water from water_tube to tf_L2_tube. Note: Brings mix to 360 uL. [assumed]
15. Mix tf_L2_tube by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. Note: Resuspend pellet. [assumed]
16. MANUAL: Incubate tf_L2_tube at 42 C for 40 min. Plate on SC-Ura, then verify SC.L2 clones by colony PCR and Sanger sequencing; prepare competent cells of SC.L2 for the next step (loss of pCRCT plasmid between steps not specified).
17. Transfer 250 µL of 50% PEG3350 from peg_tube to tf_L3_tube. Note: Step 3 transformation (SC.L2 to SC.L3, 308::mysA + mysB). [assumed]
18. Transfer 36 µL of 1 M LiAc from liac_tube to tf_L3_tube. [assumed]
19. Transfer 10 µL of 2 mg/mL ssDNA from ssdna_tube to tf_L3_tube. [assumed]
20. Transfer 1 µL of pCRCT-308a (250 ng) from pCRCT_308a_tube to tf_L3_tube. [assumed]
21. Transfer 4 µL of Linear mysA+mysB insert (2000 ng) from insert_mysAB_tube to tf_L3_tube. [assumed]
22. Transfer 59 µL of Water from water_tube to tf_L3_tube. [assumed]
23. Mix tf_L3_tube by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. [assumed]
24. MANUAL: Incubate tf_L3_tube at 42 C for 40 min. Plate on SC-Ura, verify SC.L3 clones by colony PCR and Sanger sequencing; prepare competent cells of SC.L3.
25. Transfer 250 µL of 50% PEG3350 from peg_tube to tf_L6_tube. Note: Step 4 transformation (SC.L3 to SC.L6, NQM1::mysB + mysA, NQM1 disrupted). [assumed]
26. Transfer 36 µL of 1 M LiAc from liac_tube to tf_L6_tube. [assumed]
27. Transfer 10 µL of 2 mg/mL ssDNA from ssdna_tube to tf_L6_tube. [assumed]
28. Transfer 1 µL of pCRCT-NQM1 (250 ng) from pCRCT_NQM1_tube to tf_L6_tube. [assumed]
29. Transfer 4 µL of Linear mysB+mysA insert (2000 ng) from insert_mysBA_tube to tf_L6_tube. [assumed]
30. Transfer 59 µL of Water from water_tube to tf_L6_tube. [assumed]
31. Mix tf_L6_tube by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. [assumed]
32. MANUAL: Incubate tf_L6_tube at 42 C for 40 min. Plate on SC-Ura, verify SC.L6 clones by colony PCR and Sanger sequencing; prepare competent cells of SC.L6.
33. Transfer 250 µL of 50% PEG3350 from peg_tube to tf_L26_tube. Note: Step 5 transformation (SC.L6 to SC.L26, YP::mysC). [assumed]
34. Transfer 36 µL of 1 M LiAc from liac_tube to tf_L26_tube. [assumed]
35. Transfer 10 µL of 2 mg/mL ssDNA from ssdna_tube to tf_L26_tube. [assumed]
36. Transfer 1 µL of pCRCT-YP (250 ng) from pCRCT_YP_tube to tf_L26_tube. [assumed]
37. Transfer 4 µL of Linear mysC insert (2000 ng) from insert_mysC_tube to tf_L26_tube. [assumed]
38. Transfer 59 µL of Water from water_tube to tf_L26_tube. [assumed]
39. Mix tf_L26_tube by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. [assumed]
40. MANUAL: Incubate tf_L26_tube at 42 C for 40 min. Plate on SC-Ura, verify SC.L26 clones by colony PCR and Sanger sequencing. Streak verified SC.L2, SC.L3, SC.L6 and SC.L26 colonies for pre-cultures.
41. Transfer 3000 µL of SC-Ura medium (12 g/L glucose, 8 g/L xylose) from medium_reservoir to pre_L2_tube. Note: Pre-culture volume assumed. [assumed]
42. Transfer 3000 µL of SC-Ura medium (12 g/L glucose, 8 g/L xylose) from medium_reservoir to pre_L3_tube. [assumed]
43. Transfer 3000 µL of SC-Ura medium (12 g/L glucose, 8 g/L xylose) from medium_reservoir to pre_L6_tube. [assumed]
44. Transfer 3000 µL of SC-Ura medium (12 g/L glucose, 8 g/L xylose) from medium_reservoir to pre_L26_tube. [assumed]
45. MANUAL: Inoculate one colony each of SC.L2, SC.L3, SC.L6 and SC.L26 into pre_L2_tube, pre_L3_tube, pre_L6_tube and pre_L26_tube. Incubate overnight at 30 C, 200 rpm. Measure OD600 and dilute each with medium to OD600 1.0. Note: Pre-culture and OD adjustment assumed; 30 C and 200 rpm from the published version. [assumed]
46. Transfer 180 µL of SC-Ura medium (12 g/L glucose, 8 g/L xylose) from medium_reservoir to culture_plate wells A1:D3 (each well). Note: Adapted from 100 mL in a 250 mL flask to 200 uL per well; wells A1:D3 only. [assumed]
47. Transfer 20 µL of SC.L2 pre-culture (OD600 1.0) from pre_L2_tube to culture_plate wells A1:A3 (each well). Note: Start OD600 about 0.1. [assumed]
48. Transfer 20 µL of SC.L3 pre-culture (OD600 1.0) from pre_L3_tube to culture_plate wells B1:B3 (each well). [assumed]
49. Transfer 20 µL of SC.L6 pre-culture (OD600 1.0) from pre_L6_tube to culture_plate wells C1:C3 (each well). [assumed]
50. Transfer 20 µL of SC.L26 pre-culture (OD600 1.0) from pre_L26_tube to culture_plate wells D1:D3 (each well). [assumed]
51. MANUAL: Seal culture_plate with a gas-permeable seal and incubate for 48 h at 30 C with shaking at 200 rpm. Note: Temperature and shaking from the published version; plate format assumed. [assumed]
52. MANUAL: Freeze-thaw culture_plate (whole culture, wells A1:D3) twice: -80 C then thaw at 25 C. Note: Freeze/thaw temperatures from the published version.
53. MANUAL: Centrifuge culture_plate for 10 min at the maximum speed allowed by the plate rotor (about 4000 x g). Note: Published 10000 rpm for 10 min is not possible for a plate; the plate-rotor speed is an adaptation. [assumed]
54. Transfer 150 µL of Culture supernatant from culture_plate wells A1:D3 to filter_plate wells A1:D3 (each well). Note: Well-to-well pairing in order; pellet left in the plate. [assumed]
55. MANUAL: Filter filter_plate (0.22 um) into lcms_plate wells A1:D3 by vacuum or centrifugation. Seal lcms_plate and store at -20 C until LC-HRMS. Note: Filter-plate format assumed. [assumed]
56. MANUAL: Inject 1 uL per sample (neat filtered supernatant from lcms_plate A1:D3) onto a UPLC HSS T3 column (2.5 um, 100 x 2.1 mm) at 25 C on a Vanquish Flex UHPLC with a Q-Exactive Plus (HESI, ESI+ 3.0 kV and ESI- -2.8 kV in one run, capillary and heater 350 C, sheath/aux/sweep gas 50/15/2 AU, S-lens RF 50%). Mobile phase A 0.1% formic acid in water, B acetonitrile, 0.2 mL/min; gradient: 0-5 min 1% B, 5-7 min 1-30% B, 7-9 min 30-99% B, 9-10 min 99% B, 10-10.1 min 99-1% B, 10.1-15 min 1% B. Full scan m/z 100-800 at 70,000 resolution (AGC 3e6, max injection time 100 ms); MS2 isolation window 4.0 m/z, 17,500 resolution, AGC 1e5, max injection time 50 ms, stepped NCE 30/40/50 eV, N2 collision gas. UV detection at 334 nm. Extract ion chromatograms at m/z 188.08-189.08 (4-DG, about 3.24 min) and m/z 245.10-246.10 (MG, about 2.93 min); compare relative peak areas among strains. Note: No standards described for 4-DG or MG; relative peak areas only (Fig. 2B). Shinorine and porphyra-334 standards (s4.8) are not used here.

Assumptions:
- Golden Gate details (20 uL: 1 uL BsaI-HF v2, 1 uL T4 ligase, 2 uL buffer, vector:insert 1:2, 30 cycles of 5 min 37 C / 5 min 16 C, DH5alpha transformation, Sanger verification) come from the published version (https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11983340/). The 2 uL vector, 2 uL insert and 12 uL water split is assumed. One reaction is shown and is repeated for each cassette.
- Yeast CRISPR transformation conditions (250 uL 50% PEG3350, 36 uL 1 M LiAc, 10 uL 2 mg/mL ssDNA, 250 ng pCRCT, 2000 ng linear insert, 42 C 40 min, selection on -Ura, colony PCR and Sanger) come from the published version, because refs 29 and 30 were not retrieved. Stock concentrations of 250 ng/uL (pCRCT) and 500 ng/uL (insert) and 59 uL water to reach 360 uL are assumed. Transformations are shown in 1.5 mL tubes with competent cell pellets from 5-10 mL cultures at OD600 0.5-0.8.
- Strain lineage (CEN.PK2 -> SC.L1 tal1 delta -> SC.L2 HIS3::XYL1-3 -> SC.L3 308::mysA+mysB -> SC.L6 NQM1::mysB+mysA -> SC.L26 YP::mysC) is from the published version Tables 1 and 2 (Tables are images in the preprint). SC.L1 is assumed available from an earlier TAL1 knockout. Curing of pCRCT between steps is not described.
- gRNA sequences, homology-arm lengths (about 40-500 bp), locus coordinates and PCR linearization of inserts are not stated; design 20-nt gRNAs for HIS3, 308a, NQM1 and YPRCd15c, and functionally equivalent strains are acceptable. Competent cell preparation is assumed.
- Carbon source: the preprint uses 12 g/L glucose + 8 g/L xylose, whereas the published version lists 2% glucose; the preprint medium is used here. Medium also assumed to contain the standard SC-Ura supplements; the published version's supplement mix of 1.4 g/L -His/-Trp/-Leu/-Ura plus 380 mg/L Leu, 76 mg/L Trp and 76 mg/L His can be used instead of the preprint's 1.29 g/L DO Supplement -Ura.
- Culture adapted from 100 mL SC-Ura in a 250 mL flask (30 C, 200 rpm, 48 h; published version) to 200 uL per well in a 96-well plate (180 uL medium + 20 uL inoculum), with a gas-permeable seal. Evaporation and aeration differ from flasks.
- Plate layout: culture_plate A1:A3 = SC.L2, B1:B3 = SC.L3, C1:C3 = SC.L6, D1:D3 = SC.L26 (n = 3 biological replicates each). The same layout is kept in filter_plate and lcms_plate.
- Inoculation: overnight pre-culture in 3 mL SC-Ura (30 C, 200 rpm), adjusted to OD600 1.0 and diluted 1:10 into the culture well to start OD600 about 0.1, identical for all strains (standard practice).
- Extraction: two freeze-thaw cycles at -80 C / 25 C, centrifugation and 0.22 um filtration follow the published version (10000 rpm, 10 min). Centrifugation is adapted to a plate rotor (about 4000 x g, 10 min), and 150 uL supernatant is transferred to a 0.22 um filter plate and filtered into the collection plate; both are assumptions. Samples are stored at -20 C.
- LC-HRMS parameters are as in the preprint s4.7. Neat filtered supernatant is injected; peaks are identified by EIC and MS2 and compared by relative peak area, since no 4-DG or MG standards are described. The 0.1 mg/mL shinorine and porphyra-334 standards in s4.8 are not used for this experiment, and no statistical analysis is included.
- Reagent stocks (BsaI, ligase, buffer, plasmids, inserts, PEG, LiAc, ssDNA) are assumed to be available in tubes with ample volume; container names are assumed.
