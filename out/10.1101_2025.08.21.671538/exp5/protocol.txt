BOTany6-Universal mosaic inoculation of Pichia strains in a 96 deep-well plate

Containers:
- medium_reservoir: reservoir — Bulk YP medium; supplements are added here to give YP + 1.5% methanol + 0.5% glycerol + 150 µg/mL hygromycin [assumed]
- water_reservoir: reservoir — Sterile water for 1:10 dilution of cultures before plate-reader measurement [assumed]
- methanol_tube: 15 mL tube — Methanol stock [assumed]
- glycerol_tube: 15 mL tube — Neat glycerol stock [assumed]
- hygromycin_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Hygromycin stock, 50 mg/mL [assumed]
- tube_GFP: 1.5 mL tube — Pichia sfGFP strain preculture (inoculum) [assumed]
- tube_RFP1: 1.5 mL tube — Pichia mScarlet strain RFP-1 preculture (inoculum) [assumed]
- tube_RFP2: 1.5 mL tube — Pichia mScarlet strain RFP-2 preculture (inoculum) [assumed]
- tube_RFP3: 1.5 mL tube — Pichia mScarlet strain RFP-3 preculture (inoculum) [assumed]
- tube_X33: 1.5 mL tube — Pichia X-33 (non-fluorescent) preculture (inoculum) [assumed]
- culture_plate: 96-deep-well plate (2 mL) — NEST 96-well 2 mL deep-well plate for 72 h Pichia cultivation
- readout_plate: 96-well plate — 96-well plate (black clear-bottom) holding 1:10 diluted cultures for OD600 and fluorescence reading [assumed]

Starting contents:
- medium_reservoir: 53735 µL YP medium (1% yeast extract, 2% peptone) [assumed]
- water_reservoir: 20000 µL Sterile water [assumed]
- methanol_tube: stock Methanol [assumed]
- glycerol_tube: stock Glycerol (neat) [assumed]
- hygromycin_tube: stock Hygromycin 50 mg/mL [assumed]
- tube_GFP: stock Pichia GFP strain preculture [assumed]
- tube_RFP1: stock Pichia RFP-1 strain preculture [assumed]
- tube_RFP2: stock Pichia RFP-2 strain preculture [assumed]
- tube_RFP3: stock Pichia RFP-3 strain preculture [assumed]
- tube_X33: stock Pichia X-33 strain preculture [assumed]

Steps:
1. Transfer 825 µL of Methanol from methanol_tube to medium_reservoir. Note: 1.5% (v/v) of 55 mL final bulk medium [assumed]
2. Transfer 275 µL of Glycerol (neat) from glycerol_tube to medium_reservoir. Note: 0.5% (v/v) of 55 mL; viscous, use slow aspiration/dispense [assumed]
3. Transfer 165 µL of Hygromycin 50 mg/mL from hygromycin_tube to medium_reservoir. Note: Gives 150 µg/mL in 55 mL, assuming a 50 mg/mL stock [assumed]
4. Mix medium_reservoir by pipetting 1000 µL up and down 10 times. Note: Mix supplements into bulk medium before dispensing [assumed]
5. Transfer 500 µL of YP + 1.5% methanol + 0.5% glycerol + 150 µg/mL hygromycin from medium_reservoir to culture_plate wells A1:H12 (each well). Note: All 96 wells filled, including the medium-only blank wells; fresh tip per well
6. Transfer 10 µL of Pichia GFP strain preculture from tube_GFP to culture_plate wells A1:B2, C11:D12, E9:F10, G7:H8 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed; fresh tip per well; 2x2 block mosaic layout [assumed]
7. Transfer 10 µL of Pichia RFP-1 strain preculture from tube_RFP1 to culture_plate wells A3:B4, C1:D2, E11:F12, G9:H10 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed; fresh tip per well [assumed]
8. Transfer 10 µL of Pichia RFP-2 strain preculture from tube_RFP2 to culture_plate wells A5:B6, C3:D4, E1:F2, G11:H12 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed; fresh tip per well [assumed]
9. Transfer 10 µL of Pichia RFP-3 strain preculture from tube_RFP3 to culture_plate wells A7:B8, C5:D6, E3:F4, G1:H2 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed; fresh tip per well [assumed]
10. Transfer 10 µL of Pichia X-33 strain preculture from tube_X33 to culture_plate wells A9:B10, C7:D8, E5:F6, G3:H4 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed; fresh tip per well. Blank (uninoculated) wells: A11:B12, C9:D10, E7:F8, G5:H6 [assumed]
11. MANUAL: Seal culture_plate with an air-permeable cover and incubate 72 h at 30 °C, 800 rpm, 80% humidity in a Multitron shaker (Infors HT). Note: Air-permeable seal is assumed
12. Transfer 180 µL of Sterile water from water_reservoir to readout_plate wells A1:H12 (each well). Note: Diluent for 1:10 dilution; Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025 dilute with water, generally 1:10 [assumed]
13. Mix culture_plate wells A1:H12 by pipetting 300 µL up and down 5 times. Note: Resuspend settled cells before sampling; fresh tips per well to avoid carry-over [assumed]
14. Transfer 20 µL of Pichia culture (or uninoculated medium) from culture_plate wells A1:H12 to readout_plate wells A1:H12 (each well), then mix 3 times. Note: Well-to-well pairing in order; 20 µL culture + 180 µL water = 200 µL, 1:10; fresh tip per well; post-dispense mix of about 100 µL [assumed]
15. MANUAL: Read readout_plate on a BioTek Synergy H1: OD600; GFP fluorescence ex/em 485/511 nm; RFP (mScarlet) fluorescence ex/em about 569/593 nm; same fixed gain for all wells. Note: Wavelengths from Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025 (GFP 485/511; mRuby2 569/593 used for mScarlet); gain not reported [assumed]

Assumptions:
- Supplementary Method 6 and the authors' code were not used; a generic source-to-destination transfer scheme with fresh tips per well is assumed.
- Mosaic layout (Fig 4B not described in text) is assumed: 2x2-well blocks in a Latin-square pattern, with 4 blocks (16 wells) per condition. GFP: A1:B2, C11:D12, E9:F10, G7:H8. RFP-1: A3:B4, C1:D2, E11:F12, G9:H10. RFP-2: A5:B6, C3:D4, E1:F2, G11:H12. RFP-3: A7:B8, C5:D6, E3:F4, G1:H2. X-33: A9:B10, C7:D8, E5:F6, G3:H4. Uninoculated medium-only blanks: A11:B12, C9:D10, E7:F8, G5:H6.
- The 'liquids' in the Fig 4 legend are interpreted as media and cultures; the blank wells receive medium only.
- Medium: YP base is 1% (w/v) yeast extract and 2% (w/v) peptone (Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025). Supplements are 1.5% (v/v) methanol, 0.5% (v/v) glycerol and 150 µg/mL hygromycin (s4.6). The supplements are mixed into 55 mL of bulk medium in a reservoir (about 48 mL needed plus dead-volume excess). The hygromycin stock is assumed to be 50 mg/mL, and glycerol is assumed to be neat.
- Inoculum is assumed to be 10 µL of dense preculture per well, the same for every strain. Precultures are assumed to have been grown at least 24 h in glycerol-rich medium (Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025). Preculture density is not stated in the paper.
- Strain identities (GFP, RFP-1, RFP-2, RFP-3 and X-33) are as in s4.6; the RFP-1 to RFP-3 constructs are not specified in either paper.
- The 96 deep-well plate working volume (2000 µL) accommodates the 510 µL per well. An air-permeable seal is assumed for the 72 h incubation.
- Plate-reader sample: 20 µL culture plus 180 µL water (1:10, from Fig 4 legend and Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025), 200 µL final per well. A single black clear-bottom 96-well plate is assumed to serve OD600 and fluorescence, rather than separate clear and black plates. Gain and read height are not reported, so a fixed gain is used across all wells.
- mScarlet wavelengths of about 569/593 nm are taken from the mRuby2 setting in Grieß-Osowski et al. 2025, since mScarlet settings are not reported. GFP is 485/511 nm.
- The pre-read resuspension mix of the deep-well plate is assumed, and shaking at 800 rpm continues to keep cells suspended.
- Data analysis (OD600 normalisation, cross-contamination assessment) is not included.
