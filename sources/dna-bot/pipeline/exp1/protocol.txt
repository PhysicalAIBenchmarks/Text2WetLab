DNA-BOT automated BASIC assembly and transformation of 88 three-gene operon constructs, with plate spotting

Containers:
- part_plate: 96-well plate — Source plate of BASIC DNA parts (200 ng/µl plasmids; sfGFP, mCherry, BFP, promoters, backbone), A1:H6 used as a placeholder layout [assumed]
- linker_plate: 96-well plate — Biolegio BASIC standard linker set (BBP-19100) in 96-well format; prefix half-linkers A1:H6, suffix half-linkers A7:H12 (placeholder layout) [assumed]
- t4_buffer_tube: 1.5 mL tube — 10x Promega T4 DNA ligase buffer [assumed]
- bsai_tube: 1.5 mL tube — NEB BsaI-HF v2 (R3733) [assumed]
- t4_ligase_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Promega T4 DNA Ligase (M1804) [assumed]
- water_stock: 15 mL tube — Nuclease-free H2O [assumed]
- clip_mastermix: 1.5 mL tube — Manually prepared clip reaction master mix
- clip_plate: 96-well plate — Clip reactions (30 µl each) and, subsequently, SPRI bead purification plate [assumed]
- bead_tube: 15 mL tube — AMPure XP magnetic beads
- ethanol_reservoir: reservoir — 70% ethanol for bead washes
- waste: waste container — Liquid waste (supernatants, ethanol)
- purified_clip_plate: 96-well plate — Fresh plate receiving purified clip eluates
- assembly_buffer: 1.5 mL tube — Assembly buffer (20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10 mM MgCl2, 50 mM KCl final, prepared as concentrated stock) [assumed]
- assembly_plate: 96-well plate — 15 µl BASIC assembly reactions (88 constructs, A1:H11)
- transformation_plate: 96-well plate — NEB 5-alpha Competent E. coli 96-well plate (C2987P), 20 µl cells/well
- backbone_control: 1.5 mL tube — Backbone plasmid control (BASIC_SEVA_37_CmR-p15A.1) diluted for transformation [assumed]
- soc_reservoir: reservoir — SOC outgrowth medium
- omnitray_agar: 96-well plate — Nunc OmniTray single-well plate with 40 ml LB-agar + 25 µg/ml chloramphenicol; modelled as a 96-position spotting grid (A1:H12) because no agar-plate container kind exists [assumed]

Starting contents:
- part_plate wells A1:H6: 10 µL BASIC DNA part plasmids, 200 ng/µl [assumed]
- linker_plate wells A1:H12: 20 µL Biolegio BASIC half-linkers [assumed]
- t4_buffer_tube: stock 10x Promega T4 DNA ligase buffer
- bsai_tube: stock NEB BsaI-HF v2
- t4_ligase_tube: stock Promega T4 DNA Ligase
- water_stock: stock H2O [assumed]
- bead_tube: stock AMPure XP beads
- ethanol_reservoir: stock 70% ethanol
- assembly_buffer: stock Assembly buffer (3.33x stock of 20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10 mM MgCl2, 50 mM KCl) [assumed]
- transformation_plate wells A1:H12: 20 µL NEB 5-alpha Competent E. coli [assumed]
- backbone_control: stock Backbone plasmid control, 1 ng/µl [assumed]
- soc_reservoir: stock SOC medium

Steps:
1. Transfer 165 µL of 10x Promega T4 DNA ligase buffer from t4_buffer_tube to clip_mastermix. Note: 3 µl per 20 µl x 55 reaction-equivalents (48 clips plus ~15% overage) [assumed]
2. Transfer 55 µL of NEB BsaI-HF v2 from bsai_tube to clip_mastermix. Note: 1 µl per 20 µl x 55 [assumed]
3. Transfer 27.5 µL of Promega T4 DNA Ligase from t4_ligase_tube to clip_mastermix. Note: 0.5 µl per 20 µl x 55 [assumed]
4. Transfer 852.5 µL of H2O from water_stock to clip_mastermix. Note: Remaining 15.5 µl per 20 µl of master mix is assumed to be water; 55 x 15.5 µl [assumed]
5. Mix clip_mastermix by pipetting 500 µL up and down 5 times. Note: Mix by pipetting (or gently vortex) to homogenise [assumed]
6. Transfer 20 µL of Clip master mix from clip_mastermix to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: 48 unique clip reactions is a placeholder; the real number and layout come from the construct csv files
7. Transfer 2 µL of Biolegio BASIC prefix half-linker from linker_plate wells A1:H6 to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Linker volume and pairing are not stated; 2 µl and a 1:1 well pairing are placeholders [assumed]
8. Transfer 2 µL of Biolegio BASIC suffix half-linker from linker_plate wells A7:H12 to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Volume and pairing are placeholders [assumed]
9. Transfer 1 µL of BASIC DNA part (200 ng/µl) from part_plate wells A1:H6 to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Part volume per clip is not stated; 1 µl (200 ng) is a placeholder [assumed]
10. Transfer 5 µL of H2O from water_stock to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: 20 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 5 = 30 µl [assumed]
11. MANUAL: Seal clip_plate and thermocycle in a peqSTAR 96X Universal Gradient thermocycler: 20 cycles of 37°C for 2 min and 20°C for 1 min, then 60°C for 5 min.
12. Transfer 54 µL of AMPure XP magnetic beads from bead_tube to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well), then mix 10 times. Note: Post-dispense mixing of 10 cycles is assumed
13. MANUAL: Incubate clip_plate 5 min at room temperature to bind DNA to beads, then place on magnetic stand for 3 min until the solution clears. Note: Standard SPRI practice [assumed]
14. Transfer 84 µL of Supernatant from clip_plate wells A1:H6 to waste. Note: Remove binding supernatant (30 + 54 µl) while on magnet [assumed]
15. Transfer 150 µL of 70% ethanol from ethanol_reservoir to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Wash 1 (on magnet)
16. MANUAL: Leave clip_plate on magnet 30 s. Note: Standard SPRI practice [assumed]
17. Transfer 150 µL of Ethanol wash from clip_plate wells A1:H6 to waste.
18. Transfer 150 µL of 70% ethanol from ethanol_reservoir to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Second wash; the number of washes is assumed to be two [assumed]
19. Transfer 150 µL of Ethanol wash from clip_plate wells A1:H6 to waste. [assumed]
20. MANUAL: Air-dry beads on magnet for about 3 min (do not over-dry). Note: Standard SPRI practice; 2-5 min [assumed]
21. Transfer 40 µL of H2O from water_stock to clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well), then mix 10 times. Note: Volume about 40 µl and mixing assumed so that 38 µl can be recovered [assumed]
22. MANUAL: Incubate 3 min at room temperature to elute, then return clip_plate to magnet for 2 min. Note: Standard SPRI practice [assumed]
23. Transfer 38 µL of Purified clip reaction (eluate) from clip_plate wells A1:H6 to purified_clip_plate wells A1:H6 (each well). Note: Same well positions in the fresh plate assumed
24. Transfer 4.5 µL of Assembly buffer from assembly_buffer to assembly_plate wells A1:H11 (each well). Note: Assumes 7 clips per construct (backbone, 3 promoters, 3 genes) = 10.5 µl, leaving 4.5 µl of 3.33x buffer [assumed]
25. MANUAL: OT-2 script 3 transfers 1.5 µl of each of the construct's purified clips from purified_clip_plate into its well of assembly_plate (A1:H11, 88 constructs) according to the construct layout csv. Note: Clip-to-construct mapping is in the deferred csv files and cannot be enumerated here [assumed]
26. MANUAL: Seal assembly_plate and incubate at 50°C for 45 min in a thermocycler; meanwhile thaw transformation_plate (NEB 5-alpha, stored at -80°C) on ice. Note: Thawing on ice is from the NEB protocol
27. Transfer 5 µL of Assembly reaction from assembly_plate wells A1:H11 to transformation_plate wells A1:H11 (each well). Note: 5 µl is the upper end of NEB's 1-5 µl DNA recommendation [assumed]
28. Transfer 5 µL of Backbone plasmid control from backbone_control to transformation_plate wells A12:D12 (each well). Note: Split of A12:D12 backbone and E12:H12 no-plasmid is assumed [assumed]
29. Transfer 5 µL of H2O (no-plasmid control) from water_stock to transformation_plate wells E12:H12 (each well). [assumed]
30. MANUAL: Incubate transformation_plate on ice 30 min; heat shock 10 s at 42°C; return to ice 5 min. Note: 10 s at 42°C from the NEB 96-well plate FAQ; ice times from NEB general C2987 protocol [assumed]
31. Transfer 125 µL of SOC medium from soc_reservoir to transformation_plate wells A1:H12 (each well).
32. MANUAL: Incubate transformation_plate for 1 h at 37°C with the lid off.
33. Transfer 10 µL of Transformation reaction from transformation_plate wells A1:H12 to omnitray_agar wells A1:H12 (each well). Note: 10 µl spots, as for the Figure 3b plate; spots arranged on the OmniTray in the same 8x12 layout
34. MANUAL: Allow spots to dry, incubate OmniTray inverted at 37°C overnight (about 16-18 h), then image colonies on a blue-light transilluminator. Note: Incubation conditions are standard practice; imaging per Figure 3b legend [assumed]

Assumptions:
- Heat shock: 10 s at 42°C for the NEB 5-alpha 96-well plate (NEB FAQ, https://www.neb.com/en-us/faqs/2013/03/11/what-is-the-optimal-heat-shock-time-for-the-96-well-plate-format-neb-5-alpha-competent-e-coli-neb). Cells are 20 µl per well and stored at -80°C (NEB C2987 product page).
- Ice times (30 min before, 5 min after heat shock) are from NEB's general C2987 protocol, which is written for single-tube formats.
- 5 µl of each 15 µl assembly is added to the 20 µl of cells, based on NEB's 1-5 µl DNA recommendation.
- Layout: 88 constructs in assembly and transformation wells A1:H11. Controls are in A12:H12 (the paper states this range; the split of A12:D12 backbone plasmid and E12:H12 no-plasmid water is assumed).
- Backbone control: BASIC_SEVA_37_CmR-p15A.1 diluted to about 1 ng/µl (assumed), 5 µl per well. No-plasmid control is 5 µl H2O (assumed).
- Clip reaction plate: the paper does not give the number of unique clip reactions. A placeholder of 48 clips (A1:H6) is used, and the true layout comes from the deferred csv files (storch_et_al_constructs.csv, BIOLEGIO_BASIC_STD_SET.csv, part_plate_2_230419.csv).
- Per-clip volumes of linker and part are not stated (gap in the sources). Placeholders are 2 µl of each half-linker and 1 µl (200 ng) of part, with 5 µl H2O to reach 30 µl. Linker and part plate wells are paired 1:1 with clip wells as a placeholder.
- Master mix: the stated amounts (3 µl buffer, 1 µl BsaI, 0.5 µl ligase) are per 20 µl. The remaining 15.5 µl is assumed to be H2O. 55 reaction-equivalents are made (48 clips plus about 15% overage).
- Assembly: each construct is assumed to use 7 clips (backbone, 3 promoters, 3 genes) at 1.5 µl each (10.5 µl), plus 4.5 µl of 3.33x buffer so final conditions are 20 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.0, 10 mM MgCl2, 50 mM KCl in 15 µl. The clip-to-construct transfer is a manual step because the mapping lives in the deferred csv files and DNA-BOT_instructions_v1.0.0.
- SPRI details are standard practice: 5 min binding, 3 min magnet, two 70% ethanol washes of 150 µl, 3 min drying, 40 µl H2O resuspension, 3 min elution, 38 µl transferred to a fresh plate. 10 mixing cycles on bead addition and resuspension are assumed.
- The OmniTray with LB-agar and 25 µg/ml chloramphenicol is modelled as a plate_96 container because no agar-plate kind exists. Spots are placed in a 96-position grid matching the transformation plate. 10 µl spots follow the Figure 3b plate (the standard fourth script spots 5 µl).
- After spotting, incubation is assumed to be 37°C overnight inverted (standard practice).
- Deferred methods (DNA-BOT_instructions_v1.0.0 deck setup) were not available and the robot deck set-up and script parameters are not specified. Steps are written as a generic liquid-handling sequence.
- Reagent stock tubes (ligase buffer, BsaI, ligase, H2O, beads, ethanol, SOC) are assumed to have ample volume (null).
