Robotic pipetting-speed spot growth assay of yeast (OT-2, 50/130/210/290 µL/s) with time-lapse scanning

Containers:
- culture_rep1: 50 mL tube — 30 mL W303-1B culture, biological replicate 1 (deck 7, A4)
- culture_rep2: 50 mL tube — 30 mL W303-1B culture, biological replicate 2 (deck 7, B4)
- water_tube: 50 mL tube — 50 mL Milli-Q water (deck 7, B3)
- a620_plate: 96-well plate — Corning 3997 96-well polypropylene plate for A620 reading (deck 11 on PlateShuttle)
- dilution_plate: 96-deep-well plate (2 mL) — Stand-in for the 24 x 2 mL screw-cap tubes in the deck 8 rack; one well per sample tube, A1:D6 [assumed]
- spot_plate: 96-well plate — Spotting wells holding diluted yeast, A1:D6, from which spots are drawn [assumed]
- ring_filler_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Diluted culture for the 24 outer-ring filler spots per plate [assumed]
- agar_plate_deck6: 96-well plate — Stand-in for the YPD agar microplate Petri dish on deck 6; spots are mapped to a 6x8 grid, wells A1:F8 [assumed]
- agar_plate_deck9: 96-well plate — Stand-in for the YPD agar microplate Petri dish on deck 9; spots are mapped to a 6x8 grid, wells A1:F8 [assumed]

Starting contents:
- culture_rep1: 30000 µL S. cerevisiae W303-1B culture in YPD (3 h subculture)
- culture_rep2: 30000 µL S. cerevisiae W303-1B culture in YPD (3 h subculture)
- water_tube: 50000 µL Milli-Q water
- agar_plate_deck6 wells A1:F8: stock YPD agar surface (dried 2 d) [assumed]
- agar_plate_deck9 wells A1:F8: stock YPD agar surface (dried 2 d) [assumed]

Steps:
1. MANUAL: Prepare YPD liquid medium: dissolve 2% Peptone, 1% Yeast Extract and 0.003% Adenine in 450 mL deionized water (YP) and separately prepare 500 mL of 20% dextrose in deionized water. Cover the bottles with foil and autoclave at 120 °C for 2 h. Add 50 mL of the 20% dextrose to 450 mL YP.
2. MANUAL: Prepare YPD agar: dissolve 2% Peptone, 1% Yeast Extract, 2% Agar and 0.003% Adenine in 450 mL deionized water, autoclave at 120 °C for 2 h, then add 50 mL of 20% dextrose. Pour into microplate Petri dishes on a clean bench (about 35-40 mL per dish) and leave on the clean bench for 2 d to harden and dry the surface. Weigh each dish for the tip-height calibration. Note: Pour volume and weighing are assumptions (Taguchi et al. 2023 preprint). [assumed]
3. MANUAL: Streak W303-1B wild-type from the -80 °C freeze stock onto a YPD agar plate and incubate 2 d at 30 °C.
4. MANUAL: Add 3 mL YPD to each of two autoclaved test tubes, inoculate each with a yeast colony (one per tube) and incubate in a shaking water bath at 30 °C, 160 rpm for 16 h. These are the two biological replicates. Note: A single colony per tube is assumed.
5. MANUAL: For each replicate, inoculate 3 mL of overnight culture into 27 mL fresh YPD in a 250 mL Erlenmeyer flask and shake at 30 °C, 160 rpm for 3 h. Transfer the 30 mL of each culture to a 50 mL tube (culture_rep1, culture_rep2). Note: Culture tubes then hold 30 mL each, as loaded on deck 7.
6. MANUAL: Load the OT-2 deck: deck 4 300 µL tips; deck 5 20 µL tips; deck 6 and deck 9 YPD agar microplate Petri dishes (lids off); deck 7 tube rack with culture rep1 (A4), culture rep2 (B4), Milli-Q water (B3); deck 8 rack with 24 empty 2 mL screw-cap tubes; deck 11 96-well plate on PlateShuttle. Set tip height for spotting from the plate weight: Z = 2 mm dish bottom + agar height + 1 mm clearance. Note: Tip height calculation is from the Taguchi et al. 2023 preprint. Detailed deck design is deferred to Experimental Design.pdf. [assumed]

Assumptions:
- A620 target 0.020 for normalising cell density, read in a 96-well plate: taken from Taguchi et al. 2023 (bioRxiv 10.1101/2022.07.16.500326), not stated in this paper.
- A620 well: 180 µL H2O + 20 µL culture (200 µL), with H2O-only blank wells H1:H3 subtracted (Taguchi et al. 2023). Mix 3 x 100 µL before reading is assumed.
- The A620 reading is done in the Byonoy Absorbance 96 reader, via PlateShuttle or by hand. PlateShuttle operation is deferred to GitHub.
- Dilution volumes are nominal: 20 µL culture + 280 µL water (300 µL) per 2 mL tube. In practice use the per-sample volumes calculated from A620. The paper does not state them.
- The 24 x 2 mL screw-cap tubes are represented by wells A1:D6 of a deep-well plate (dilution_plate).
- Pipette: the paper lists two P20 entries but also 300 µL tips, so a P300 is assumed for volumes over 20 µL. Spots use a P20 with 20 µL tips.
- The test speed (50/130/210/290 µL/s, aspirate and dispense) is applied to culture to A620 wells, culture to 2 mL tubes, and 2 mL tubes to spotting wells. Water dispensing, mixing, spotting onto agar and filler transfers use the default OT-2 flow rate. Speed is given in step notes because the schema has no speed field.
- Design inferred: 24 samples = 4 speeds x 2 biological replicates x 3 technical replicates, with the same 24 samples spotted on both plates (4x6 inner spots each) plus a 24-spot outer ring (6x8 grid).
- Sample layout (well: speed-biorep), in spotting order: A1 50-1, A2 130-2, A3 210-1, A4 290-2, A5 50-2, A6 130-1, B1 210-2, B2 290-1, B3 290-1, B4 210-2, B5 130-1, B6 50-2, C1 290-2, C2 210-1, C3 130-2, C4 50-1, C5 130-1, C6 290-2, D1 50-1, D2 210-2, D3 130-2, D4 290-1, D5 50-2, D6 210-1. This ordering is my own interleaving; the paper's agar_plate_annotation.csv is deferred.
- Agar plates are represented as 96-well plates (A1:F8 = 6x8 spot grid); inner spots B2:E7 are filled row by row from top to bottom in sample order, and the outer ring is A1:A8, F1:F8, B1:E1 and B8:E8.
- The spotting wells (spot_plate A1:D6) hold 50 µL per sample; spots of 5 µL are drawn from there. Both volumes are assumed (5 µL spot from Taguchi et al. 2023).
- Filler for the outer ring: 40 µL rep 1 culture + 560 µL water (nominal), spotted after the inner spots on each plate; plates are spotted sequentially, deck 6 then deck 9.
- Culture resuspension before aspiration: mix 5 x 200 µL, assumed.
- Agar pour volume (about 35-40 mL per dish) and weighing are assumed. Tip Z = 2 mm dish bottom + agar height + 1 mm clearance (Taguchi et al. 2023; agar density 0.00107 g/mm³, 86 mm dish diameter in that paper, to be re-measured for these dishes).
- Single colony used to inoculate each overnight culture; the paper does not state colony size.
- Scanner: 30 °C, colour/RGB, plates agar-side down aligned to scanner corners (Taguchi et al. 2023 used the same scanner and a 30 °C incubator; this paper scans every 10 min rather than 90 min).
- Analysis (baQFA, ANOVA, RNA-seq sample preparation) is excluded; the protocol covers spotting and scanning only.
