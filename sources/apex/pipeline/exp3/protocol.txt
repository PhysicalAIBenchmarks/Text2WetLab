Validation of colony sampling, microculturing and sfGFP expression in E. coli BL21(DE3) (APEX Protocols 1-4)

Containers:
- competent_cells_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Calcium-competent BL21(DE3) cells in 0.1 M CaCl2 + 15% glycerol, kept on ice [assumed]
- pEX05_dna_tube: 1.5 mL tube — sfGFP plasmid pEX05 (pJKR-H-araC) at 1.5e-4 pmol/µL [assumed]
- pEX01_dna_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Positive-control plasmid pEX01 at 1.5e-4 pmol/µL [assumed]
- soc_tube: 15 mL tube — SOC recovery medium [assumed]
- lb_reservoir: reservoir — Plain LB (diluent for spot dilution, OD readout and blanks) [assumed]
- lb_carb_reservoir: reservoir — LB + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin for overnight cultures and subcultures [assumed]
- arabinose_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Sterile 100 mM L-arabinose stock [assumed]
- transform_plate: 96-well plate — Thermocycler PCR plate for heat shock transformation; columns 1-8 = pEX05 (sfGFP), column 9 = pEX01 control [assumed]
- dilution_plate: 96-well plate — Plate for 5-fold dilution of transformations spotted at low density (columns 5-8) [assumed]
- agar_plate: 96-well plate — Nunc OmniTray with LB agar + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin, 8x12 spot grid (modelled as a 96-position plate; agar not liquid) [assumed]
- overnight_plate: 96-deep-well plate (2 mL) — 96 deep-well plate for overnight cultures inoculated from sampled colonies [assumed]
- od_plate: 96-well plate — Clear 96-well plate for 1:10 OD600 readout of overnight cultures, with LB blanks in column 10 [assumed]
- subculture_plate: 96-deep-well plate (2 mL) — 96 deep-well plate for back-diluted subcultures, induced with L-arabinose [assumed]
- reader_plate: 96-well plate — Clear-bottom black 96-well plate for the Varioskan LUX time course

Starting contents:
- competent_cells_tube: 800 µL BL21(DE3) calcium-competent cells [assumed]
- pEX05_dna_tube: stock pEX05 plasmid (sfGFP, pJKR-H-araC), 1.5e-4 pmol/µL [assumed]
- pEX01_dna_tube: stock pEX01 plasmid, 1.5e-4 pmol/µL [assumed]
- soc_tube: stock SOC medium
- lb_reservoir: stock LB medium
- lb_carb_reservoir: stock LB medium + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin [assumed]
- arabinose_tube: stock L-arabinose 100 mM [assumed]
- agar_plate wells A1:H12: stock LB agar (1.5%) + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin [assumed]

Steps:
1. MANUAL: Prepare Hanahan calcium-competent BL21(DE3): inoculate a single colony in 5 mL LB, grow overnight at 37 °C with shaking; dilute 100-fold into 50 mL LB and grow at 37 °C, 300 rpm to OD600 = 0.4; chill on ice 20 min; pellet at 4000 ×g, 4 °C, 10 min; resuspend in 2 mL 0.1 M CaCl2 + 15% (v/v) glycerol; store at -80 °C. Thaw on ice before use (commercial Thermo BL21(DE3) cells may be substituted). Note: Can be skipped if competent cells are already available
2. Transfer 10 µL of BL21(DE3) competent cells from competent_cells_tube to transform_plate wells A1:H9 (each well). Note: LV configuration assumed for Fig. 5; 72 transformations (8 rows x 9 columns) [assumed]
3. Transfer 1 µL of pEX05 plasmid (1.5e-4 pmol) from pEX05_dna_tube to transform_plate wells A1:H8 (each well). Note: sfGFP plasmid in columns 1-8 [assumed]
4. Transfer 1 µL of pEX01 plasmid (1.5e-4 pmol) from pEX01_dna_tube to transform_plate wells A9:H9 (each well). Note: Positive control in column 9 [assumed]
5. MANUAL: Seal transform_plate and run in the thermocycler: 4 °C for 30 min, then heat shock at 42 °C for 30 s (rapid ramp), returning immediately to 4 °C. Note: Return to 4 °C hold is assumed
6. Transfer 50 µL of SOC medium from soc_tube to transform_plate wells A1:H9 (each well). Note: 50 µL from LV configuration [assumed]
7. MANUAL: Incubate transform_plate in the thermocycler at 37 °C for 1 h for recovery.
8. MANUAL: Weigh the empty and the agar-filled OmniTray (about 30-35 mL LB agar + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin, assumed). Agar height = (plate+agar weight - empty plate weight) / (base area x 0.911 g/cm3). Set the spotting height to about 1-2 mm above the calculated agar surface (assumed offset). Note: Density 0.911 g/cm3 for rectangular plates [assumed]
9. Transfer 40 µL of LB diluent from lb_reservoir to dilution_plate wells A5:H8 (each well). Note: Diluent assumed LB [assumed]
10. Transfer 10 µL of Recovered transformation from transform_plate wells A5:H8 to dilution_plate wells A5:H8 (each well), then mix 5 times. Note: 10 µL into 40 µL = 5-fold dilution [assumed]
11. Transfer 5 µL of Recovered transformation (undiluted) from transform_plate wells A1:H4 to agar_plate wells A1:H4 (each well). Note: High density spots
12. Transfer 5 µL of Recovered pEX01 transformation (undiluted) from transform_plate wells A9:H9 to agar_plate wells A9:H9 (each well). Note: Control spotted undiluted (assumed) [assumed]
13. Transfer 5 µL of 5-fold diluted transformation from dilution_plate wells A5:H8 to agar_plate wells A5:H8 (each well). Note: Low density spots
14. MANUAL: Let spots dry, then incubate the agar plate inverted at 37 °C for about 16 h until colonies form. Note: Time and temperature not stated in paper [assumed]
15. Transfer 500 µL of LB + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin from lb_carb_reservoir to overnight_plate wells A1:H9 (each well). Note: Volume assumed (0.5 mL) [assumed]
16. MANUAL: Single pierce sampling: with a fresh tip per spot, lower the tip to the centre of each spot in agar_plate columns 1-4 (high density) and column 9 and just into the agar, then withdraw. Note: Pierce depth assumed; column 9 sampled by pierce (assumed) [assumed]
17. Mix overnight_plate wells A1:H4, A9:H9 by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. Note: Mix with the same tip that sampled the colony to release cells [assumed]
18. MANUAL: Spiral sampling: with a fresh tip per spot, start at the centre of each spot in agar_plate columns 5-8 (low density) and trace slow, smooth spirals outward (radius about 1-2 mm, about 2-3 rotations, about 8-16 points), keeping the tip just below the agar surface. Note: Spiral geometry values assumed [assumed]
19. Mix overnight_plate wells A5:H8 by pipetting 200 µL up and down 10 times. Note: Mix with the sampling tip [assumed]
20. MANUAL: Seal overnight_plate with a gas-permeable membrane and incubate overnight (about 16-18 h) at 37 °C with vigorous shaking (about 250 rpm). Note: Conditions not specified in paper [assumed]
21. Transfer 180 µL of LB from lb_reservoir to od_plate wells A1:H9 (each well). Note: 1:10 dilution for OD readout (assumed) [assumed]
22. Transfer 200 µL of LB blank from lb_reservoir to od_plate wells A10:H10 (each well). Note: Blank wells [assumed]
23. Transfer 20 µL of Overnight culture from overnight_plate wells A1:H9 to od_plate wells A1:H9 (each well), then mix 3 times. [assumed]
24. MANUAL: Read OD600 of od_plate in a plate reader; subtract LB blank and correct for the 1:10 dilution. [assumed]
25. Transfer 490 µL of LB + 100 µg/mL carbenicillin from lb_carb_reservoir to subculture_plate wells A1:H9 (each well). Note: Volume assumed [assumed]
26. Transfer 10 µL of Overnight culture from overnight_plate wells A1:H9 to subculture_plate wells A1:H9 (each well), then mix 3 times. Note: 1:50 back-dilution assumed [assumed]
27. MANUAL: Seal subculture_plate with a gas-permeable membrane and manually transfer to a shaking incubator at 37 °C (about 250 rpm) until OD600 is approximately 0.2 (about 2-3 h; check OD600 by manual read). Note: Time and shaking speed assumed [assumed]
28. Transfer 6.7 µL of L-arabinose 100 mM from arabinose_tube to subculture_plate wells A1:H8 (each well), then mix 3 times. Note: 6.7 µL of 100 mM into about 500 µL gives about 1.33 mM final; column 9 receives no inducer (negative control) [assumed]
29. Transfer 150 µL of Subculture (induced columns 1-8; uninduced column 9) from subculture_plate wells A1:H9 to reader_plate wells A1:H9 (each well).
30. MANUAL: Incubate reader_plate in a Varioskan LUX at 37 °C with shaking at 960 rpm (high speed); measure OD600 and sfGFP fluorescence (excitation 485 nm, emission 528 nm) every 10 min for 4 h; report fluorescence (AFU) normalised to OD600.

Assumptions:
- Layout: transform_plate, agar spots, overnight, subculture and reader plates all use A1:H9 with the same mapping: columns 1-8 = pEX05 (sfGFP), column 9 = pEX01 positive control; 8 rows = 8 replicates per column (assumed). Columns 1-4 undiluted spots sampled by single pierce (high density); columns 5-8 5-fold diluted spots sampled by spiral (low density). Column 9 receives no L-arabinose (negative control).
- Transformation volumes: LV configuration (10 µL cells, 1 µL DNA at 1.5e-4 pmol, 50 µL SOC) taken from the Fig. 4 legend; the paper does not state Fig. 5 used exactly these. Heat shock 4 °C 30 min, 42 °C 30 s, SOC recovery 37 °C 1 h are the defaults from s2.2.
- Competent cells prepared by Hanahan method per s4.2; any wash assumed to use ice-cold 0.1 M CaCl2; commercial Thermo BL21(DE3) is an alternative. Competent-cell tube volume of 800 µL is assumed.
- Antibiotic: pJKR-H-araC (pEX05) is ampicillin resistant (pUC backbone, Addgene 62563); carbenicillin 100 µg/mL used in LB agar and liquid LB (s4.1). pEX01 antibiotic is in an image-only table, assumed also carbenicillin-resistant.
- The agar OmniTray is a solid plate, not a liquid container; it is modelled as a plate_96 (8x12 spot grid) with agar spots at A1:H12 positions. Agar volume (about 30-35 mL), carbenicillin in agar, and spot height offset (1-2 mm above agar) are assumed. Agar density 0.911 g/cm3 for rectangular plates (s2.3).
- Spot diluent for 5-fold dilution assumed to be LB (not stated); pEX01 control in column 9 spotted undiluted and sampled by single pierce (assumed).
- Agar plate incubation after spotting (about 16 h at 37 °C) is assumed; paper does not state it.
- Spiral and pierce geometry (radius 1-2 mm, 2-3 rotations, 8-16 points, depth, speed), fresh tip per spot, and mixing the sampling tip in 500 µL LB-carbenicillin (200 µL mix volume, 10 cycles) are assumed.
- Overnight culture: 500 µL LB-carbenicillin per deep well, gas-permeable seal, 16-18 h at 37 °C, about 250 rpm; all assumed (paper says only 'overnight culturing').
- OD600 of overnight cultures: 1:10 dilution in LB (20 µL + 180 µL) in a clear 96-well plate with LB blanks in column 10; dilution correction applied (assumed; Protocol 7 details are in the supplement).
- Subculture: 10 µL overnight into 490 µL LB-carbenicillin (1:50) in deep well plate, grown at 37 °C with shaking until OD600 about 0.2 (about 2-3 h, assumed); s4.5 uses back-dilution to OD600 0.1 for the related experiment.
- Induction: 100 mM sterile L-arabinose stock assumed; 6.7 µL per 500 µL gives about 1.33 mM final (s4.4). Induction is done in the subculture plate before transferring 150 µL to the reader plate (assumed ordering).
- Plate-reader setup (150 µL per well, black clear-bottom plate, Varioskan LUX, 37 °C, 960 rpm, 485/528 nm, every 10 min for 4 h) from s4.4.
- Details of Protocols 2-4 are in Supplementary File 1, not available; values taken from the recovered gap assessment (Addgene 62563 page and paper-internal values) and standard lab practice.
