Steps:

2. MANUAL: Seal sample_plate, heat 2 min at 80 °C, then short vortex mixing; unseal.
3. MANUAL: Bring Mag-Bind TotalPure NGS beads and MagMAX CORE beads to room temperature and vortex to resuspend; prepare 70% ethanol fresh with nuclease-free water into etoh_reservoir; mix Lysis and Binding solutions 1:1 into lysis_binding_reservoir (350+350 µL per 700 µL). Note: Bead handling and Omega buffer composition from cited manufacturer sources. [assumed]
4. Transfer 1936 µL of MagMAX CORE Magnetic Beads from kit_beads_tube to bead_pk_mix. Note: 20 µL x 88 samples x 1.1 overage. [assumed]
5. Transfer 968 µL of MagMAX CORE Proteinase K from kit_pk_tube to bead_pk_mix. Note: 10 µL x 88 x 1.1. [assumed]
6. Mix bead_pk_mix by pipetting 1000 µL up and down 5 times. Note: Mix volume and cycles assumed. [assumed]
7. Transfer 500 µL of MagMAX CORE Wash Solution 1 from kit_wash1_stock to mmkit_wash1 wells A1:H11 (each well).
8. Transfer 500 µL of MagMAX CORE Wash Solution 2 from kit_wash2_stock to mmkit_wash2 wells A1:H11 (each well).

10. Transfer 30 µL of MagMAX CORE beads/PK mix from bead_pk_mix to mmkit_deep wells A1:H11 (each well). Note: Order bead/PK, sample, Lysis/Binding follows the MagMAX CORE user guide.
11. Transfer 200 µL of Inactivated sample from sample_plate wells A1:H11 to mmkit_deep wells A1:H11 (each well).

20. MANUAL: Load mmin_wash1, mmin_wash2, mmin_elute and mmin_deep into the MagMAX Express-96 and run script MagMAX_CORE_KF-96 (5 min room temperature binding is part of the run); eluate ends in mmin_elute. Note: Script internals not described; Thermo script used unmodified. [assumed]

22. Transfer 250 µL of Isopropanol from ipa_reservoir to ot2_deep wells A1:H11 (each well).

Assumptions:
- Scope: the 141 samples are laid out as one 88-sample batch (wells A1:H11) per method so that each qPCR plate holds 88 samples + positive control (A12) + NTC (B12). The remaining 53 samples would be a second batch with identical steps; not enumerated.
- Samples are assumed to be transferred from swab tubes into the deep well plate sample_plate (500 µL VTM per well); the paper does not state the sample container.
- Inactivation, in the paper done per tube, is done in the deep well plate (500 µL VTM + 500 µL 4 M GTC with 5 µg/mL carrier RNA, 80 °C for 2 min, short vortex). Carrier RNA is assumed to be at 5 µg/mL in the GTC reagent; the paper is unclear whether this is the stock or final concentration.
- Each well keeps 1000 µL inactivated sample, enough for 200 (MMkit) + 250 (OT-2) + 500 µL (MMin-house) in parallel from the same sample.
- Mock-sample validation with the TaqMan 2019-nCoV Control Kit (extraction efficiency, s2.4) is outside the listed sections and is not included; only the clinical-sample arm is laid out. No extraction negative control well is included; qPCR NTC wells are used.
- MMkit: per the MagMAX CORE user guide, bead/PK mix is 20 µL beads + 10 µL Proteinase K per sample (10% overage), added first (30 µL), then 200 µL sample, then 700 µL Lysis/Binding. The paper states 300+300 µL Lysis/Binding but the user guide states 350+350 µL; the 700 µL total of Table 1 is used.
- KingFisher/MagMAX Express-96 runs use the Thermo script MagMAX_CORE_KF-96 unmodified; script internals (step times, mixing, heating) are not stated and were not retrieved. Wash 1/2 plates are deep well plates and the elution plate is a standard MagMAX Express 96 plate; a tip comb is assumed to be used.
- MMin-house and OT-2in-house use Mag-Bind TotalPure NGS beads at room temperature, vortexed before use, with isopropanol (He et al. adaptation); 70% ethanol is made fresh, assumed with nuclease-free water. Omega Elution Buffer is 10 mM Tris-HCl pH 8.5.
- OT-2: paper-stated parameters used (40 µL beads, 250 µL isopropanol, 250 µL sample, 5 pipette mixes, 5 min room temperature, 4 min magnet, two 500 µL 70% ethanol washes, 4 min air dry, 100 µL elution, 30 s off magnet, 90 s on magnet, transfer to a 96-well plate). Mix volume (200 µL), supernatant removal volumes (540 µL then 500 µL), 100 µL eluate transfer volume, pipetting heights/speeds, tip use are assumed. The OT-2 script is on GitHub and was not available.
- Waste transfers use the waste container with empty dest wells.
- qPCR (TaqMan 2019-nCoV Assay Kit v1 manual, 'conditions recommended by the manufacturer'): per 20 µL reaction 6.25 µL 4X master mix + 1.25 µL assay (orf1ab, S or N) + 1.25 µL RNase P assay + 11.25 µL RT-PCR grade water, plus 5 µL sample (25 µL total). TaqPath 1-Step RT-qPCR Master Mix CG is assumed because the paper does not name the master mix. One master mix per assay prepared for 297 reactions (270 + 10% overage).
- qPCR layout: one 96-well plate per method per assay (9 plates); A1:H11 samples (same well position as the eluate plate), A12 positive control (1 µL Control v1 + 4 µL water, prepared as 10 + 40 µL in one tube), B12 NTC (5 µL RT-PCR grade water). The paper dispensed eluates into qPCR strips with another OT-2 module; plates are used here. Cycling: 25 °C 2 min, 50 °C 15 min, 95 °C 2 min, 40 cycles of 95 °C 3 s / 60 °C 30 s; FAM/VIC; positive Ct < 37; sample positive if at least 2 of 3 assays positive (manual).
- Reservoirs/tubes of reagents (GTC, beads, isopropanol, ethanol, wash solutions, buffers, master mix, assays) are assumed to be stocks with ample volume (null volume); tube kinds are assumed. Premixed Lysis/Binding solution is treated as an initial reagent (assumed).
- Mixing of bead/PK mix and qPCR master mixes (1000 µL x 5 cycles) is assumed; the paper does not state it.
