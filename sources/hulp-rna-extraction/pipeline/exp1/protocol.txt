Assumptions:
- Mock samples laid out in deep-well plate mock_plate: A1:A3 standard, A4:A6 low, A7:A9 very-low, A10 negative control (VTM + GTC + water, no control DNA). One 1000 uL mock well per replicate supplies all three methods (200 + 250 + 500 uL).
- The same mock sample layout (A1:A10) is used in every method plate and eluate plate. qPCR plates: row A MMkit, row B MMin-house, row C OT-2in-house (columns 1-10); D1:D3 control reference (1e4, 1e3, 1e2 copies/uL), D4 NTC.
- Ten-fold serial dilutions of the control (ctrl_1e3, ctrl_1e2) were made in nuclease-free water (diluent not stated in paper).
- The paper's arithmetic (10 uL x 1e4 copies/uL in 1 mL) gives 1e5 copies/mL, not the stated 1e6 copies/mL; the stated procedure was followed and efficiency should use actual copies added.
- Mock samples receive the same GTC (with 5 ug/mL carrier RNA), 80 °C 2 min and short vortex as clinical samples; mock plate mix volume 500 uL x 5 cycles assumed.
- MMkit Lysis/Binding: paper text says 300+300 uL but Table 1 says 700 uL; used 350 uL Lysis + 350 uL Binding per sample from the MagMAX CORE user guide (MAN0015944). Beads/PK mix 20+10 uL per sample with 10% overage; kit volumes (30 uL beads/PK, 200 uL sample, 700 uL lysis/binding, 500 uL washes, 90 uL elution) follow the guide and Table 1.
- KingFisher script MagMAX_CORE_KF-96 run with default vendor settings (times, mixing, heating not given); identical script for MMkit and MMin-house. Tip comb and plate loading order per vendor guide.
- 70% ethanol prepared fresh as 17.5 mL absolute ethanol + 7.5 mL water (25 mL; ~20 mL needed for both in-house arms).
- OT-2 parameters not given in paper (pipette/tip type, rates, heights, magnet height): assumed default Opentrons P300/P1000 settings; mix volume 200 uL; supernatant removal 540 uL; bead resuspension in elution buffer by pipetting 5x; eluate transfer volume 100 uL; the authors' GitHub scripts were not used.
- OT-2in-house run on deep-well plate on the GEN1 magnetic module; eluate transferred to a standard 96-well plate.
- Reagents in stocks (ctrl_stock, VTM, GTC, kit reagents, beads, IPA, etc.) assumed to be in ample volume (volume null).
- qPCR master mix not named in paper: TaqPath 1-Step RT-qPCR Master Mix, CG assumed; per reaction 6.25 uL 4x mix + 1.25 uL 2019-nCoV assay + 1.25 uL RNase P assay + 11.25 uL water + 5 uL template = 25 uL, with 10% overage (38 reactions per assay) per TaqMan 2019-nCoV Assay Kit v1 manual (MAN0019096). Each of the ORF1ab, S and N assays is run in duplex with RNase P on its own plate.
- Cycling conditions from MAN0019096 for TaqPath 1-Step master mix (25 °C 2 min; 50 °C 15 min; 95 °C 2 min; 40 x 95 °C 3 s / 60 °C 30 s). Positive call Ct < 37 and auto baseline / fixed threshold per target on CFX Maestro are analysis-side assumptions.
- Reference (Ctpc) wells: 1 uL of control stock or dilution + 4 uL water per well, run once per plate; expected copies are to be corrected afterwards for each method's input volume (200, 250, 500 uL) and elution volume (90 or 100 uL). Replicates of reference wells not stated.
- Eluate dispensed into a qPCR 96-well plate (5 uL) instead of strips, by OT-2 or by hand; the paper used qPCR strips with another OT-2 module.
- Waste container used for discarded supernatant and ethanol; waste transfers use no destination wells.
- Tubes for beads/PK, lysis/binding, ethanol and qPCR mixes are assumed containers; deep-well plate capacity (2000 uL) suffices for the largest well volume (1040 uL).
