Food dye color-gradient mock sitting drop plate on the OT-2 vs manual pipetting (24-well CrysChem format)

Containers:
- reservoir_plate: 96-deep-well plate (2 mL) — Hampton CrysChem 24-well plate (HR3-159) on the 3D-printed adapter. Moat reservoirs are mapped to wells A1:D6 (4 rows x 6 columns). A 400 µL fill exceeds the 360 µL plate_96 working capacity, so a deep-well container stands in for the CrysChem plate. [assumed]
- pedestal_plate: 96-well plate — Raised center pedestals (sitting drop wells) of the same CrysChem plate, mapped to wells A1:D6. Each pedestal sits inside the matching reservoir well of reservoir_plate. Modeled as a separate container only to track contents. [assumed]
- water_tube: 15 mL tube — DI water for filling reservoirs to 400 µL (about 6 mL needed) [assumed]
- red_dye_tube: 15 mL tube — Pure red food dye stock (about 1.8 mL needed) [assumed]
- blue_dye_tube: 15 mL tube — Pure blue food dye stock (about 1.8 mL needed) [assumed]
- yellow_dye_tube: 1.5 mL tube — Yellow dye stock standing in for purified protein (about 120 µL needed plus dead volume) [assumed]

Full text (PMC12229254) could not be retrieved, so Fig. 3 and Supp. Fig. 3 details are assumed.
- Container adaptation: the CrysChem 24-well plate is not an available container kind. Moat reservoirs are modeled as wells A1:D6 of a plate_96_deep container (400 µL exceeds the 360 µL plate_96 capacity). Pedestals are modeled as wells A1:D6 of a separate plate_96 container (10 µL).

- Water is added first, then red, then blue, then mixed (order is standard practice). Dyes and water go into the moat, not the pedestal. Water is split into per-well transfers because volumes differ per well.
- Reservoir mixing parameters are assumed because the paper defers them to Results, which was not provided: p300, 200 µL mix volume, 4 cycles (range 3-5), tip about 1-2 mm above the moat bottom, default flow rates, slow dispense.

- Dye identity and concentration are not given (only 'pure' red and blue stocks and a 'yellow dye stock solution'). Assumed commercial liquid food dyes in DI water, giving clear color at 30-150 µL in 400 µL. The same stocks are used for the robot and manual plates. Stocks are set to null (ample) volume. Needed amounts: about 1.8 mL red, 1.8 mL blue, 6 mL water, 120 µL yellow.

The p300 (200 µL tips) handles about 30 µL and above and splits volumes over 200 µL into multiple aspirations. The p10 handles the 5 µL yellow and 5 µL reservoir aspirations into one tip.
