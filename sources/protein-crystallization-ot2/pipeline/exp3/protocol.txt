Assumptions:
- The CrysChem 24-well plate is not an available container kind. Each plate is modeled as two containers: a deep-well plate (reservoir moats, 400 µL exceeds the 360 µL plate_96 capacity) and a 96-well plate (pedestals), both using wells A1:D6 (rows A–D, columns 1–6). Physically they are one plate.
- Only one OT-2 plate and one human plate are written out as templates. The full comparison used 3 OT-2 plates (the original plus two additional) and 9 human plates (3 people with varying wet-lab experience, read as 3 plates each); repeat the template for each (source: full text of the published article, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2472630325000263).
- HR2-805 composition (30% w/v PEG MME 5,000, 1.0 M NaCl, 0.05 M sodium acetate pH 4.6) is from https://hamptonresearch.com/product-Lysozyme-524.html and the HR7-108 user guide.
- The 4 µL drop is 2 µL protein plus 2 µL reservoir because a 2 µL total drop could not be reliably ejected by the Gen1 p10 (full text of the published article). In the OT-2 run both are aspirated in the same p10 tip and dispensed together; this is represented as two transfers per well.
- HEWL reservoirs needed no mixing step on the OT-2 (full text of the published article), so no mix steps are included. The food-dye script's 200 µL aspirate/redispense at 3 and 9 o'clock is skipped.
- Water volumes per column (50, 40, 30, 20, 10, 0 µL) are calculated as 400 µL − HR2-805 − 50 µL acetate; column 6 needs none.
- Layout: rows A–D, columns 1–6. HR2-805 rises from 300 to 350 µL by column; sodium acetate pH 4.6 in row A, pH 4.7 in row B, pH 4.8 in rows C and D.
- Paired transfers from reservoir wells to pedestal wells assume row-major pairing of A1:D6 to A1:D6 (each well's reservoir goes to its own pedestal).
- OT-2 pipetting parameters are not stated; assumed default flow rates, p10 Gen1 for the drops, p300 Gen2 for reservoir volumes (the 10 µL water steps could use the p10), a fresh tip per liquid or well, and tip-touch on the well wall at 12, 3, 6 and 9 o'clock (gap-assessment recommendations).
- Custom labware: the paper gives only the A1 offset (x 30.5, y 12.5, z 6.1 mm) and plate size (150×106 mm). Build an equivalent labware definition, use a 3D-printed PLA adapter clipped into two deck slots (the paper's printed in 84 min on a Bambu X1C), and verify well positions before using protein.
- Manual (human) procedure is not described in the paper. Assumed: same layout and volumes as the OT-2 plate, manual P10/P200 pipettes, gentle swirl of the plate, and experience level recorded per person without further definition.
- Setup time is assumed to run from the start of reservoir preparation to the last drop dispensed, before taping. The OT-2 plate took 31 min (about 12 min for drops). Human times are in Supplemental Fig. 5, which was not retrieved.
- Incubation at room temperature (about 20–22 °C) for 24 h is assumed; the paper gives only 24 h. Use the same location for all plates.
- Crystal scoring (present/absent per well at 24 h under a stereomicroscope, same observer, ideally blinded) is assumed. The paper reports 18/24 wells with crystals for the OT-2 plate.
- Lysozyme stock: dissolve HR7-110 to 20 mg/mL in 20 mM sodium acetate pH 4.6, mix gently until clear, spin or filter if cloudy, and use one stock for both arms (standard practice). Tubes and the water reservoir are assumed containers; stock volumes are given as null (ample).
