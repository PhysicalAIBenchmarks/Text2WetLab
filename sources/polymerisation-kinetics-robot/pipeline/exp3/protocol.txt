Assumptions:
- The 48-well reactor is not an available container kind, so it is represented by a plate_96_deep (2000 µL per well) because 400 µL reactions exceed the 360 µL plate_96 capacity. Only wells A1:F5 (45 wells) and A1:C4 (20 wells) are used.
- Cut glass NMR tubes in the 3D-printed holder are represented as plate_96_deep wells at the same positions as the reactor wells (t0 and t1 racks per run).
- Two runs: Run 1 = 30 min time control (20 reactions: f10 = 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8 x AIBN 0.05, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 wt%); Run 2 = 45-point extended set at 2 h (f10 = 0.1-0.9 in 0.1 steps x 5 AIBN levels). The 45 = 9 x 5 split (SI Table S2 rows 1-20 and 61-85) is reconstructed from the main text; Table S2 was not consulted. The 20-point 2 h set is contained in run 2.
- Combining datasets (56 or 79 points) and the JCI comparison are post-hoc analysis and are not included. The text gives both 56 and 79 as totals, which cannot be reconciled.
- Well layout is explicit and non-overlapping. The paper randomises well positions by script; here a fixed pseudo-random layout is used (well index = 7 x condition number mod number of conditions, filling rows A-F across 8 columns), recorded for traceability.
- Monomers and AIBN are dispensed as toluene stock solutions. The paper does not say whether neat or stock solutions were used. Stock concentrations of 4 M BMA and 4 M BA in toluene are assumed. 200 µL of combined monomer stocks gives 0.8 mmol in 400 µL, i.e. 2 M total monomer, as stated.
- Inhibitor is assumed removed from monomers (basic alumina plug), as standard practice.
- AIBN stocks are in toluene at 2.7, 13.5, 27, 54 and 108 mg/mL; 20 µL is dispensed to give 0.05, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0 wt% relative to total monomer. The calculation assumes an average monomer mass of about 108 mg per 400 µL (MW BMA 142.2, BA 128.17), so the actual wt% varies by about ±4% across f10. The highest AIBN stock may be near its solubility limit in toluene, so check dissolution.
- Toluene (180 µL) fills each well to 400 µL. Order of addition: toluene, AIBN, BA, BMA, then mix. Mix 150 µL x 3 cycles is assumed.
- No deoxygenation is performed. The paper polymerises through oxygen using high AIBN loadings. The reactor is sealed with strong compressive force; the exact seal type is not given, so a compressive sealing lid is assumed.
- Heater-shaker: 75 °C stated in Table 1; 2 h or 30 min stated; shaking speed (about 400 rpm), warm-up and a cooling time of about 10 min before unsealing are assumed.
- NMR samples: 20 µL aliquot into 580 µL CDCl3 per tube (600 µL total) is assumed. The paper does not give the solvent, aliquot volume, or any quenching/inhibitor. Each aliquot removes 20 µL, so the reactor volume is 380 µL at t1. Conversion is a ratio, so exact dilution does not matter.
- Glass NMR tubes are cut with a glass tube cutter to clear the tip-loaded pipette heads, and a 3D-printed NMR tube holder is used (Fig. S1, S2), as stated in the paper.
- No external sources could be fetched; all details come from the paper text and standard lab practice. NMR acquisition settings and peak integration are not included.
