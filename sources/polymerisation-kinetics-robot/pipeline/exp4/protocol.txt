Assumptions:
- Total monomer concentration is 2 M in toluene, 400 µL per well (s2.3). The 4 M in s2.5 is treated as a probable typo.
- Each of the six pairs is run as one 20-reaction experiment in its own 48-vial reactor, as in s2.3. Pairs and monomer indexing follow Table 2 (M1 listed first).
- f10 = 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8 and AIBN = 0.05, 0.25, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0 wt% of total monomer are used for all pairs, copied from Table 1 (BMA-BA). SI Tables S4-S8 were not available.
- The 48-vial reactor is modelled as a plate_96_deep container (positions A1:F8) because 400 µL exceeds the 360 µL plate_96 working capacity. The reactor model, vials and seal are not stated in the paper.
- The paper randomises well positions with a Python script. Here a fixed pseudo-random layout of 20 wells is used for every pair: A2,A5,A7,B1,B4,B6,B8,C3,C7,C8,D2,D5,D8,E1,E4,E6,F2,F3,F5,F7. Each f10 x AIBN combination occupies one well.
- Layout by f10: 0.1 = A5,B4,C7,D8,F2; 0.3 = A7,B8,D2,E4,F5; 0.5 = B1,C3,D5,E6,F7; 0.8 = A2,B6,C8,E1,F3.
- Layout by AIBN: 0.05 wt% = A5,B6,D2,E6; 0.25 wt% = B1,C7,E1,F5; 0.5 wt% = A2,B8,D5,F2; 1 wt% = B4,C8,E4,F7; 2 wt% = A7,C3,D8,F3.
- Monomers are used as 4 M stocks in toluene (BMA, BA, St, MMA, GMA), prepared manually after passing the neat monomers through basic alumina to remove inhibitor (standard practice; gap assessment). 200 µL of monomer stock plus 200 µL of AIBN solution/toluene gives 2 M in 400 µL. Stock volumes (12 mL each) are an assumed excess.
- Monomer volumes per well: M1 = 20, 60, 100, 160 µL and M2 = 180, 140, 100, 40 µL for f10 = 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.8.
- AIBN stock design is assumed (the paper gives only wt% of total monomer; gap assessment says to prepare AIBN in toluene with several concentrations). Stocks of 10.8, 5.4, 2.7, 1.35 and 0.27 mg/mL are made by weighing 108 mg AIBN into 10 mL toluene and diluting on deck (1:2, 1:4, 1:8, 1:40).
- The stocks are sized for a reference monomer mass of 108 mg per 400 µL well (0.8 mmol x mean MW at f10 = 0.5). For lighter pairs the AIBN volume is reduced and made up to 200 µL with toluene: BMA-BA and GMA-BA 200 µL, BMA-St and GMA-St 182 µL (+18 toluene), St-BA 172 µL (+28), St-MMA 151 µL (+49).
- The MW used were BMA 142.2, GMA 142.15, BA 128.17, St 104.15, MMA 100.12. A single nominal AIBN volume per pair is used, so actual wt% varies by roughly +/-10% with f10 within a pair. The paper's script computes exact volumes per well from the csv.
- Order of dispensing (toluene, AIBN, M1, then M2 with 3 post-dispense mix cycles) is assumed. Each well is mixed on its M2 addition. Mixes are pipette-mix cycles, which is assumed. Pipette type, aspiration speed, pre-wet and air gap are set in the OT-2 script using standard practice for volatile liquids (gap assessment). Dispensing is kept quick to limit the time wells stay unsealed (s2.1).
- No separate deoxygenation is performed. High AIBN loadings and compression sealing are used (s2.3, s2.1; gap assessment).
- Sealing: the reactor is sealed under strong compression (s2.1). The seal type is not stated. Sealing is done after t0 sampling and before heating.
- Polymerisation is at 75 °C for 2 h (s2.3, Table 2). The heater-shaker speed of about 500-800 rpm and the cooling after the reaction are assumed (gap assessment).
- NMR sampling: 15 µL aliquots from each reactor well at t0 and t1 are diluted into 585 µL CDCl3 (about 0.6 mL total) in cut NMR tubes held in a custom holder. Volumes are assumed per gap assessment (standard practice). The holder is modelled as a plate_96_deep because a 600 µL tube exceeds plate_96 capacity. t0 samples use A1:A12 and B1:B8, t1 samples use C1:C12 and D1:D8, paired in order with the reactor well list.
