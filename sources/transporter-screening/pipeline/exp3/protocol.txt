Assumptions:
- Source: https://www.mdpi.com/2079-6382/11/8/1129 Section 2.2/Table 1 and Section 4 used for MIC definition (lowest concentration with OD600 at 48 h <10% of the drug-free LB control), WT MICs, 48 h read, and the plate format (CR1496c plates, 3 µL culture:50% glycerol pre-load, 297 µL LB+drug, CR1396b covers, Growth Profiler 960 at 37 °C, 225 rpm, images every 20 min).
- The MIC-specific plate set-up (Supplementary Figure S1) was not available; the screen format was assumed.
- Layout: three deep-well dilution plates (dil_D1-D3), each with 6 drugs in rows A-F; columns 1-10 = two-fold dilution series (col 1 = top concentration, col 10 = top/512), column 11 = drug-free LB (growth control), column 12 = LB blank (no inoculum; used as Gblank/sterility control). Rows G-H unused.
- Each dilution plate is dispensed into three separate CR1496c growth plates (replicates r1-r3; n = 3 per the Fig. 7A legend, assumed for all drugs), giving 9 plates, which fit within the 10-plate capacity of the Growth Profiler 960. Growth plates have the same A1:F12 layout as the dilution plate.
- Top concentrations (mg/L): azithromycin 480, cefiderocol 16, cefoperazone 8, chloramphenicol 40, ceftriaxone 40, D-cycloserine 200, flumequine 1.5, gentamicin 64, levofloxacin 4, meropenem 1, ofloxacin 2, ornidazole 3200, paraquat 268, phosphomycin 800, rifampicin 400, streptomycin 400, trimethoprim 36.8, zidovudine 100. For most drugs this is 16 x the Table 1 MIC (about 4 steps above and 5 below MIC); ornidazole is capped at 4 x MIC for solubility; flumequine, paraquat and zidovudine use the tabulated top concentration because they still grew at the highest concentration tested (Table 1).
- Top-concentration solutions (1800 µL each, in LB, filter-sterilised) are assumed to be prepared beforehand from Sigma stocks (rifampicin, ceftriaxone and azithromycin from TCI) dissolved in LB or water, or a minimal volume of the recommended solvent (final solvent <=1%, constant across wells); the concentrated stock preparation is not modelled as pipetting steps.
- Serial dilution is performed in deep-well plates: 900 µL transferred each step (multiple aspirations with the 300 µL pipette), 5 mix cycles per step (assumed); 900 µL removed from column 10 so all wells hold 900 µL, which is enough for 3 x 297 µL.
- LB for dilution is drawn from a single reservoir; total LB needed is about 215 mL, within the 290 mL capacity.
- WT overnight culture: 10 µL WT BW1556 glycerol stock into 1 mL LB (volume assumed), overnight growth at 37 °C with agitation (225 rpm and ~16 h assumed); no OD standardisation of the inoculum, as in the paper's screen workflow. Two wells (A1, A2) are used because 9 plates x 66 wells x 3 µL (about 1.8 mL) approaches the 2 mL well capacity.
- Pre-inoculated growth plates carry 3 µL culture:glycerol in wells A1:F11 only (not in column 12 blank or rows G-H). They are stored at -20 °C at least overnight (duration assumed) as in the paper, then thawed before adding 297 µL LB+drug (thawing step assumed).
- Final volume per well is 300 µL (3 µL + 297 µL), within the 360 µL well capacity.
- Replicate numbers for MICs other than paraquat are not stated; triplicate assumed.
- The Figure 6 legend (400 mg/L azithromycin) conflicts with Table 1 (7.5 mg/L screening dose); Table 1 MIC of 30 mg/L was used to design the dilution range. The paraquat text (67 mg/L screen) vs Fig. 7 legend (32 mg/L) inconsistency does not affect the series, which spans 268 to about 0.5 mg/L.
- Plates are manually sealed and loaded; data analysis (MIC calling, Growthcurver fits, choice of MIC/2 screening dose) is excluded as analysis.
