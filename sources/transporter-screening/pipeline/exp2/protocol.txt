8. Transfer 5 µL of Glycerol stock of library plate 3 from stock_plate_3 wells A1:H12 to culture_plate_3 wells A1:H12 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed [assumed]
9. Transfer 5 µL of Glycerol stock of library plate 4 from stock_plate_4 wells A1:H12 to culture_plate_4 wells A1:H12 (each well). Note: Inoculum volume assumed [assumed]

13. Transfer 1000 µL of 50% glycerol from glycerol_reservoir to culture_plate_2 wells A1:H12 (each well), then mix 5 times. Note: Mix cycle number assumed [assumed]
14. Transfer 1000 µL of 50% glycerol from glycerol_reservoir to culture_plate_3 wells A1:H12 (each well), then mix 5 times. Note: Mix cycle number assumed [assumed]

31. Transfer 146853 µL of LB from LB_reservoir to abx_LB_reservoir_B. Note: Bulk antibiotic-LB for replicate B plates [assumed]

Assumptions:
- Scope: the full screen uses 18 antimicrobials plus an antibiotic-free LB condition, each on 5 library plates in duplicate (10 Growth Profiler plates per condition). Only one condition, azithromycin 7.5 mg/L, is written out. Repeat the antibiotic-LB preparation and plate-filling steps for each other compound at its Table 1 screening concentration (mg/L: cefiderocol 0.25, cefoperazone 0.25, chloramphenicol 1.25, ceftriaxone 1, D-cycloserine 6.25, flumequine 1.5, gentamicin 2, levofloxacin 0.125, meropenem 0.03125, ofloxacin 0.0625, ornidazole 400, paraquat 67, fosfomycin 25, rifampicin 12.5, streptomycin 6.25, trimethoprim 0.25, zidovudine 100) (https://www.mdpi.com/2079-6382/11/8/1129 Table 1).
- Table 1 values are used where legends disagree: azithromycin 7.5 mg/L (Fig 6 legend says 400 mg/L) and paraquat 67 mg/L (Fig 7 legend says 32 mg/L).
- Azithromycin was sourced from Tokyo Chemical Industries; Sigma supplied the other compounds except rifampicin and ceftriaxone, which also came from TCI.
- 'Lucia Broth (LB)' is assumed to be standard Lennox LB (10 g/L tryptone, 5 g/L yeast extract, 5 g/L NaCl), autoclaved, no supplements (https://www.sigmaaldrich.com/US/en/product/sigma/l3022).
- Antibiotic stock: assumed 1000x stock (7.5 mg/mL azithromycin) in DMSO, or the minimum solvent needed, filter-sterilised and diluted 1:1000 into LB (final solvent 0.1%, at most 1% v/v). A matching solvent control is advised but not included. One bulk antibiotic-LB per replicate set (5 plates x 96 x 297 µL = 142.56 mL, made up to 147 mL with about 3% overage); reservoir capacity is 290 mL.
- Library layout: the 5 library plates (447 Keio transporter knockouts plus BW1556 WT; refs 19 and 20 in the paper) are laid out A1:H12; well H12 is assumed empty in each stock plate and acts as the uninoculated blank for G_blank. WT wells are assumed present on every plate. Replicate A and replicate B are separate full sets of 5 plates (10 plates in the Growth Profiler 960). Keep a well-to-strain map. Missed-inoculation wells (growth in one replicate only) are excluded in analysis.
- Glycerol stock to deep-well plate inoculum volume (5 µL) is not stated and is assumed. Overnight culture assumed 16-20 h at about 220-250 rpm; OD was not measured or normalised. Final 25% glycerol after mixing gives 2 mL per deep well, at the plate working capacity.
- Mix cycles after glycerol addition (5) and after antibiotic addition to the reservoir (10 at 1000 µL) are assumed.
- The 3 µL culture:glycerol mix is dispensed with the OT-2 20 µL multichannel pipette (touching the tip to the well bottom so the droplet is deposited); 297 µL antibiotic-LB with the 300 µL multichannel. Assume a fresh tip per column or reservoir fill, default OT-2 flow rates, gentle dispense, no mixing. Scripts at github.com/ljm176/TransporterScreening were not consulted.
- Plates are assumed thawed at room temperature before LB addition; the paper does not say whether they are thawed.
- Sterilisation: the manufacturer lists CR1496c polystyrene plates as single-use gamma-sterilised and not autoclavable, so fresh plates are assumed (https://www.enzyscreen.com/product/polystyrene-transparent-square-96-half-deepwell-microplates-CR1496c/). If reused, wash, rinse and UV-treat. CR1396b covers can be autoclaved wrapped for 20-30 min at 120 °C with a dry cycle (or oven-dried 1 h at 100-110 °C), or the seal wiped with water-ethanol; no dishwasher (https://www.enzyscreen.com/article/maintenance-sterilisation-and-use-of-sandwich-covers/).
- Run duration 48 h (about 145 images) is assumed from the 48 h MIC readout. The OD conversion uses the paper's a = 0.0158 and b = 0.9854, so no new calibration is needed (https://www.enzyscreen.com/strain-calibration/).
- Volumes up to 146.9 mL are moved reservoir to reservoir as single transfers for simplicity; in practice they are poured or pumped. LB and glycerol reservoirs are treated as ample stocks and refilled as needed.
- The WT MIC determination (two-fold dilution in LB, OD at 48 h, MIC <10% of antibiotic-free OD) and data analysis are not part of this protocol.
