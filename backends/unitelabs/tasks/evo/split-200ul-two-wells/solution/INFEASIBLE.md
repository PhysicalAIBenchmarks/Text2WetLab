# Infeasible on the Tecan Freedom EVO (MCA96)

Step 1 (100 µL from `reservoir` into `plate` wells A1 and B1 only) cannot be done as specified.
The MCA96 head always picks up and uses all 96 tips and moves the same volume in every tip; it cannot address just two wells.
Every dispense into the plate would put 100 µL into all 96 wells, not only A1 and B1.
