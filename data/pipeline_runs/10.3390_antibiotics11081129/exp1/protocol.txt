Assumptions:
- 'Lucia Broth' is assumed to be a typo for Luria-Bertani broth: standard LB (10 g/L tryptone, 5 g/L yeast extract, 10 g/L NaCl), autoclaved, no antibiotic.
- Glycerol stock plates are assumed to be 96-well plates (5 plates, wells A1:H12), with the layout of the 447 strains, WT and blank wells not specified in the paper; unused wells (480 - 447) are assumed to hold WT controls or sterile/blank wells. The same A1:H12 layout is carried through all plates.
- Inoculum volume from glycerol stock to each overnight well is not stated; 5 µL assumed (standard practice), transferred with a fresh tip per transfer.
- Overnight growth assumed to be about 16 h at 37 °C with about 220 rpm shaking and a gas-permeable seal; the paper states only 'overnight', 37 °C and agitation.
- Mixing the overnight culture with 1 mL of 50% glycerol assumed to be done by 5 pipette cycles at about 500 µL; 1 mL + 1 mL = 2 mL fills the deep well to its working capacity.
- Each deep-well plate seeds two assay plates (replicate 1 and 2) with 3 µL per well, so the 5 library plates give 10 assay plates (library in duplicate, as in the paper). The 3 µL droplets are dispensed at the well bottom with the 20 µL multichannel pipette (OT-2); 297 µL LB with the 300 µL multichannel pipette. Default OT-2 speeds and a fresh tip per transfer assumed; the paper's scripts were not consulted.
- The assay plate CR1496c is a 96-half-deepwell polystyrene plate with a recommended culture volume of 0.3 mL (Enzyscreen, https://www.enzyscreen.com/product/polystyrene-transparent-square-96-half-deepwell-microplates-CR1496c/); it is represented here as plate_96 (360 µL working capacity), which fits the 300 µL final volume.
- No separate thawing or mixing of frozen assay plates is described; 297 µL LB is added to the stored plates directly and the droplet dissolves during robot dispensing and Growth Profiler shaking (standard practice).
- LB reservoir and 50% glycerol reservoir are given as stocks of ample volume (volume null); total needs (about 480 mL LB for overnights, about 285 mL LB for assay, about 480 mL glycerol) exceed one reservoir's capacity, so they are assumed to be refilled during the run.
- Assay run duration is not stated; about 24 h (until all wells reach stationary phase) assumed, with the same window used for AUC for all strains. Growth Profiler settings (37 °C, 225 rpm, 20 min imaging) are from the paper.
- Blank wells for G_Blank (uninoculated LB) are assumed to be included in the layout, and G-values are computed with the manufacturer's software; OD600 = 0.0158*(G - G_Blank)^0.9854 as given in the paper, so the standard curve is not redone.
- Plate sterilisation between uses ('washing and UV in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions') is deferred: assume fresh gamma-sterilised CR1496c plates, or wash, dry and UV-sterilise reused plates; cycle time not given.
- Growth rate (r) and AUC are obtained afterwards by Growthcurver in R; this analysis step is not included in the liquid-handling protocol.
- Details drawn from the paper's s4 and Enzyscreen pages (https://www.enzyscreen.com/article/technical-specifications/, https://www.enzyscreen.com/product/universal-sandwich-cover-for-96-well-mtps-stackable/).
