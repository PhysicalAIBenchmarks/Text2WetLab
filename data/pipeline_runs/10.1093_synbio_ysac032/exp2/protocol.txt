Golden Gate assembly of four four-fragment chromoprotein expression plasmids with AssemblyTron on the OT-2

Containers:
- water: 50 mL tube — Nuclease-free molecular grade water [assumed]
- q5_buffer: 1.5 mL tube — 5X Q5 reaction buffer (NEB) [assumed]
- dntp: 1.5 mL tube — 10 mM dNTP mix [assumed]
- q5_pol: 1.5 mL tube — Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase (NEB) [assumed]
- pcr_mm: 1.5 mL tube — PCR master mix (buffer, dNTPs, polymerase, water) for 8 reactions [assumed]
- primer_plate: 96-well plate — j5-designed primers at 1 µM working stock: forward primers in A1:G1, reverse primers in A2:G2 (fragments 1-7) [assumed]
- template_plate: 96-well plate — Linearized template plasmids diluted to 0.5 ng/µL [assumed]
- pcr_plate: 96-well plate — PCR / DpnI / fragment plate (stands in for 100 µL PCR tubes in the thermocycler block); column 1 wells A1:G1 = fragments 1-7 [assumed]
- rcutsmart: 1.5 mL tube — rCutSmart Buffer (NEB)
- dpni: 1.5 mL tube — DpnI (NEB)
- t4_buffer: 1.5 mL tube — 10X T4 DNA Ligase Buffer [assumed]
- gg_enzyme: 1.5 mL tube — Golden Gate Enzyme Mix (BsaI-HFv2 + T4 DNA ligase) [assumed]
- assembly_plate: 96-well plate — Golden Gate reactions A1:D1 (on Opentrons thermocycler module) [assumed]
- cells_plate: 96-well plate — Chemically competent E. coli TOP10 for transformation, A1:D1 [assumed]
- lb_dextrose: 15 mL tube — LB + 0.2% (w/v) dextrose (LB with catabolite repression) recovery medium

Starting contents:
- water: stock nuclease-free water [assumed]
- q5_buffer: stock 5X Q5 reaction buffer [assumed]
- dntp: stock 10 mM dNTPs [assumed]
- q5_pol: stock Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase [assumed]
- primer_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1, G1: 50 µL forward primers for fragments 1-7 (1 µM) [assumed]
- primer_plate wells A2, B2, C2, D2, E2, F2, G2: 50 µL reverse primers for fragments 1-7 (1 µM) [assumed]
- template_plate well A1: 20 µL pIDMv5K-J23100-tsPurple-B1006 linearized, 0.5 ng/µL [assumed]
- template_plate well B1: 20 µL pIDMv5K-J23100-YukonOFP-B1006 linearized, 0.5 ng/µL [assumed]
- template_plate well C1: 20 µL pIDMv5K-J23100-aeBlue-B1006 linearized, 0.5 ng/µL [assumed]
- template_plate well D1: 20 µL pIDMv5K-J23100-fuGFP-B1006 linearized, 0.5 ng/µL [assumed]
- rcutsmart: stock rCutSmart Buffer
- dpni: stock DpnI
- t4_buffer: stock 10X T4 DNA Ligase Buffer [assumed]
- gg_enzyme: stock Golden Gate Enzyme Mix (BsaI-HFv2 + T4 ligase) [assumed]
- cells_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1: 50 µL E. coli TOP10 chemically competent cells (Hanahan method)
- lb_dextrose: stock LB + 0.2% (w/v) dextrose

Steps:
1. Transfer 106 µL of nuclease-free water from water to pcr_mm. Note: Master mix for 8 reactions (7 fragments + 1 overage); Q5 chosen; Q5 M0491 composition. [assumed]
2. Transfer 40 µL of 5X Q5 reaction buffer from q5_buffer to pcr_mm. Note: 5 µL per 25 µL reaction x 8 [assumed]
3. Transfer 4 µL of 10 mM dNTPs from dntp to pcr_mm. Note: 0.5 µL per reaction x 8 (200 µM final) [assumed]
4. Transfer 2 µL of Q5 High-Fidelity DNA Polymerase from q5_pol to pcr_mm. Note: 0.25 µL per reaction x 8 [assumed]
5. Mix pcr_mm by pipetting 100 µL up and down 5 times. Note: Mix master mix [assumed]
6. Transfer 19 µL of PCR master mix from pcr_mm to pcr_plate wells A1:G1 (each well). Note: One PCR per fragment, fragments 1-7 in A1:G1. [assumed]
7. Transfer 2.5 µL of forward primer (1 µM) from primer_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1, G1 to pcr_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1, G1 (each well). Note: 2.5 µL of 1 µM in 25 µL = 0.1 µM final [assumed]
8. Transfer 2.5 µL of reverse primer (1 µM) from primer_plate wells A2, B2, C2, D2, E2, F2, G2 to pcr_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1, G1 (each well). Note: 2.5 µL of 1 µM in 25 µL = 0.1 µM final [assumed]
9. Transfer 1 µL of linearized template plasmid (0.5 ng/µL) from template_plate wells A1, A1, B1, C1, A1, A1, D1 to pcr_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1, E1, F1, G1 (each well). Note: Layout assumed: F1 tsPurple, F3 YukonOFP, F4 aeBlue, F7 fuGFP gene; backbone/KanR fragments F2, F5, F6 from the tsPurple plasmid. Check against Figure 3B / Table S1. [assumed]
10. Mix pcr_plate wells A1:G1 by pipetting 15 µL up and down 3 times. Note: Mix 25 µL PCRs [assumed]
11. MANUAL: Seal pcr_plate (or move the PCR tubes) and transfer manually to the Bio-Rad C100 gradient thermocycler. Run: 98°C 30 s; 34 cycles of 98°C 10 s, annealing 30 s at the AssemblyTron/j5 optimal-gradient temperature for each fragment, 72°C extension at the time set by AssemblyTron (about 20-30 s/kb); final extension 72°C 5 min; hold 4°C. Take a sample of each reaction for gel electrophoresis, then return to the OT-2. Note: Annealing temps and extension times come from the AssemblyTron algorithm; not given in the paper. [assumed]
12. Transfer 19 µL of nuclease-free water from water to pcr_plate wells A1:G1 (each well).
13. Transfer 5 µL of rCutSmart Buffer from rcutsmart to pcr_plate wells A1:G1 (each well).
14. Transfer 1 µL of DpnI from dpni to pcr_plate wells A1:G1 (each well).
15. Mix pcr_plate wells A1:G1 by pipetting 30 µL up and down 3 times. Note: Mix 50 µL DpnI reactions [assumed]
16. MANUAL: Incubate pcr_plate A1:G1 at 37°C for 30 min, then 65°C for 20 min (DpnI inactivation) on the thermocycler block.
17. MANUAL: Pause the protocol. Clean and concentrate each of the 7 fragments with a Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrator-5 column: 5:1 DNA Binding Buffer to sample (250 µL per 50 µL), spin 30 s, wash 2 x 200 µL DNA Wash Buffer (30 s spins), elute in 20 µL water after 1 min at room temperature (30 s spin). Return the eluted fragments to their original positions pcr_plate A1:G1 (fragments 1-7) and resume the protocol. Note: Fragment elution volume not stated; 20 µL assumed. Zymo manual volumes. [assumed]
18. Transfer 7 µL of nuclease-free water from water to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: Water brings the 20 µL Golden Gate reaction to volume; four assemblies A1 tsPurple, B1 YukonOFP, C1 aeBlue, D1 fuGFP. [assumed]
19. Transfer 2 µL of 10X T4 DNA Ligase Buffer from t4_buffer to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: NEB E1601 composition. [assumed]
20. Transfer 3 µL of fragment 2 (backbone) from pcr_plate well B1 to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: Volumes are placeholders (about 0.05 pmol each, scaled by fragment length); replace with length-proportional volumes from the AssemblyTron CSVs / Figure 3B. [assumed]
21. Transfer 2 µL of fragment 5 (backbone/KanR) from pcr_plate well E1 to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: Placeholder volume proportional to length. [assumed]
22. Transfer 3 µL of fragment 6 (backbone) from pcr_plate well F1 to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: Placeholder volume proportional to length. [assumed]
23. Transfer 2 µL of chromoprotein fragments 1, 3, 4, 7 from pcr_plate wells A1, C1, D1, G1 to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: Fragment 1 to A1, 3 to B1, 4 to C1, 7 to D1 (assumed pairing); placeholder volume. [assumed]
24. Transfer 1 µL of Golden Gate Enzyme Mix (BsaI-HFv2 + T4 ligase) from gg_enzyme to assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well), then mix 5 times. Note: Enzyme added last; 20 µL total; NEB E1601 for up to 10 inserts. [assumed]
25. MANUAL: Run Golden Gate program on assembly_plate in the Opentrons thermocycler module: 30 cycles of 37°C 5 min then 16°C 5 min; then 60°C 5 min; hold at 4°C. Lid about 85°C. If no module is available, pause and move reactions to another thermocycler. Note: Program from NEB technical note; paper does not give cycling. [assumed]
26. MANUAL: Clean and concentrate each assembly with a Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrator-5 column (5:1 binding buffer, 2 x 200 µL wash) and elute in 10 µL molecular grade water; return the eluates to assembly_plate A1:D1. Note: Manufacturer volumes for binding/wash.
27. Transfer 5 µL of purified Golden Gate assembly from assembly_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 to cells_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well). Note: 5 µL of the 10 µL eluate transformed; the other 5 µL kept for NanoDrop (the 5 µL split is inferred from the text and the recovered details). [assumed]
28. MANUAL: Measure DNA concentration of the remaining 5 µL of each eluate with a NanoDrop-2000c for CFU/µg calculation.
29. MANUAL: Incubate cells_plate A1:D1 for 30 min on ice, heat shock at 42°C for 60 s, then return to ice for about 2 min. Note: 2 min on ice after heat shock is assumed. [assumed]
30. Transfer 250 µL of LB + 0.2% (w/v) dextrose from lb_dextrose to cells_plate wells A1, B1, C1, D1 (each well).
31. MANUAL: Recover cells at 37°C for 60 min with shaking (about 250 rpm). Note: Shaking speed assumed. [assumed]
32. MANUAL: Plate 50-200 µL of each recovery (neat or a 10x dilution, depending on predicted efficiency) onto separate LB agar plates containing kanamycin 50 µg/mL; incubate at 37°C overnight. Note: Kanamycin because the assembly carries a KanR gene.
33. MANUAL: Manually count colonies per plate and score the fraction showing the expected chromoprotein colour (purple, orange, blue, green); report CFU/µg of DNA plated. Note: Colour-scoring details assumed; reported result 300-800 colonies, about 10^4 CFU/µg, >98% positive. [assumed]

Assumptions:
- Layout: PCR fragments 1-7 in pcr_plate A1:G1; primers in primer_plate (forward A1:G1, reverse A2:G2); templates in template_plate A1:D1; Golden Gate reactions in assembly_plate A1:D1 (A1 tsPurple, B1 YukonOFP, C1 aeBlue, D1 fuGFP); cells in cells_plate A1:D1. Pairing of chromoprotein genes to fragment numbers 1,3,4,7 and backbone fragments 2,5,6 to templates is assumed; check against Figure 3B, Table S1 and File S7.
- PCR tubes (100 µL) in the thermocycler block are represented as a 96-well plate; PCR is run manually in the Bio-Rad C100 as in the paper.
- Q5 polymerase was chosen (paper says Phusion or Q5); Q5 M0491 composition used: 5 µL 5X buffer, 0.5 µL 10 mM dNTPs, 0.25 µL Q5 per 25 µL, water to volume, with the paper's 0.1 µM primers and 0.5 ng template. Master mix made for 8 reactions.
- Primers prepared as 1 µM working stocks (2.5 µL each gives 0.1 µM final) and templates as 0.5 ng/µL linearized stocks (1 µL gives 0.5 ng); these concentrations are assumed for pipetting practicality.
- Cycling: 34 cycles (paper says 34 or 36); annealing temperatures per fragment come from the AssemblyTron/j5 gradient and extension 20-30 s/kb (NEB Q5 M0491 and Phusion M0530 protocols); not given numerically in the paper.
- DpnI step (19 µL water, 5 µL rCutSmart, 1 µL DpnI, 37°C 30 min, 65°C 20 min) is as in s2.3; units are not stated.
- Fragment cleanup with Zymo DNA Clean & Concentrator-5 follows the manufacturer's protocol (5:1 binding buffer to PCR sample, 2 x 200 µL wash, spins 30 s); fragment elution volume of 20 µL is assumed (paper does not state it).
- Golden Gate reaction composition not stated in the paper: used NEB E1601 (BsaI-HFv2 + T4 ligase Golden Gate Enzyme Mix, 1 µL; 2 µL 10X T4 DNA Ligase Buffer; 20 µL total) as the modern equivalent of Engler et al. 2009 (BsaI inferred from the paper's mention of BsaI sites).
- Fragment volumes (3, 2, 3 µL for fragments 2, 5, 6; 2 µL per chromoprotein fragment) are placeholders approximating about 0.05 pmol each; the paper only says volume is proportional to fragment length and equal PCR yields are assumed. Use the length-proportional volumes calculated by AssemblyTron; dilute fragments first if too concentrated.
- Golden Gate program: (37°C 5 min, 16°C 5 min) x 30 then 60°C 5 min, per NEB technical note CLO_BreakingThroughLimitsGoldenGate; lid temperature about 85°C assumed. Engler 2009 used 37/16°C cycles with 50°C and 80°C final steps; the paper gives no parameters.
- Zymo cleanup of the final assembly is eluted in 10 µL water; 5 µL goes to 50 µL TOP10 cells, the other 5 µL to NanoDrop (inferred from s2.3 and recovered details).
- Transformation: 30 min ice, 42°C 60 s, 2 min ice (assumed), recovery 37°C 60 min with 250 µL LB + 0.2% dextrose, shaking about 250 rpm (assumed); 50-200 µL plated on LB agar with kanamycin 50 µg/mL, 37°C overnight; well volume 305 µL fits the 360 µL plate_96 limit.
- Competent TOP10 cells are assumed to be available (Hanahan method, about 8.47 x 10^8 CFU/µg pUC19); preparation is not part of this protocol.
- Template linearization with a restriction enzyme cutting outside the amplicon is assumed already done; the enzyme is not specified.
- Plating and colony counting are manual; plates for plating are not modelled as containers. The expected outcome is 300-800 colonies per assembly, about 10^4 CFU/µg, >98% correct by chromoprotein colour (paper, s3.2).
- Water, buffers, dNTPs, polymerase and enzymes are taken as stocks with ample volume; container kinds for them are assumed.
